# Join Clean-Up Drives and Tree Planting Activities — Cabanatuan City

Clean-up drives and tree planting in Cabanatuan City are coordinated by the Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CCENRO). The Citizen's Charter does not list a procedure for volunteering, so call or visit the office to ask about the next activity and how to join.

---

## Who to contact

**Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office**
2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 4509

Ask the office for the schedule of upcoming clean-up drives and tree planting activities, the meeting point, and whether a barangay, school or company group can register.

## Seedlings

Residents who apply to cut a tree on their own property donate seedlings as part of the clearance. See [Get a tree-cutting clearance](/services/environment/get-a-tree-cutting-clearance).
