# Get a Hauling Pass — Cabanatuan City

A City Hauling Pass gives truck owners, operators and haulers permission to haul sand and gravel from the applied area. It is issued by the Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CCENRO) for {FEE_HAULING_PASS} and released as a sticker, in {TIME_HAULING_TOTAL}. This page gives the checklist and steps, and the separate Mayor's Clearance that quarry operators need.

---

## Who may avail

Truck owners, operators and haulers, whether or not they live in Cabanatuan. The charter classifies the hauling pass as **Simple** (Government to Client).

## Where to go

**Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office** — application, inspection and release
2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 4509

**Office of the City Treasurer** — payment of the fee
1st Floor, City Hall
Phone: 0919 089 9849 or 044 464 7696

## Quarry operators: Mayor's Clearance or Permit to Quarry

If you operate a quarry, you need a Mayor's Clearance or Permit to Quarry from CCENRO before you apply to quarry. It is a **Simple** transaction (Government to Business), filed on CCENRO Application Form 2. The fee is {FEE_QUARRY}; the schedule of amounts by type of establishment is on the [City Environmental Certificate page](/services/environment/get-a-city-environment-certificate-or-permit-to-operate). Total processing time: {TIME_QUARRY_TOTAL}.

| Requirement                                                                                                                                      | Where to secure                                                                          |

| 1 piece 2x2 picture of the applicant or owner                                                                                                    | Photo shop                                                                               |

| Location map, sketch plan or survey plan of the proposed permit area showing geographic coordinates                                              | Deputized Geodetic Engineer                                                              |
| Proof of identification of the owner and the authorized representative                                                                           | Photocopy of any valid ID                                                                |
| Barangay Resolution interposing no objection                                                                                                     | Sangguniang Barangay                                                                     |
| Zoning or Locational Clearance or Certificate                                                                                                    | City Planning and Development Office                                                     |

| City Environmental Certificate                                                                                                                   | CCENRO                                                                                   |

The steps match the hauling pass: submit the form, pay at the Office of the City Treasurer, wait for the inspection and evaluation by the Ecological Management Division Officer of the Day, then the Head of CCENRO and the City Mayor approve and sign the clearance.

---

## What the charter does not cover

The charter does not state how long a hauling pass is valid, whether one sticker covers more than one truck, or the fee for the provincial hauling pass that is on the checklist. Ask the Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office at 0919 081 4509.
