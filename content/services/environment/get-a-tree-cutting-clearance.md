# Get a Tree-Cutting Clearance — Cabanatuan City

Before you cut a tree on your own property in Cabanatuan City, get a Mayor's Clearance, Certificate of No Objection or Tree Cutting Clearance from the Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CCENRO). The fee is {FEE_TREE_CUTTING}, you donate seedlings, and the charter lists {TIME_TREE_CUTTING_TOTAL} of processing time, including a site inspection.

---

## Who may avail

Applicants who wish to cut trees within their property. The charter classifies this as a **Simple** transaction (Government to Client).

## Where to go

**Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office** — application, inspection and release
2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 4509

**Office of the City Treasurer** — payment of the fee
1st Floor, City Hall
Phone: 0919 089 9849 or 044 464 7696

## What the charter does not cover

The charter covers trees within your own property only. It does not say what to do about a tree on a road, a sidewalk, a riverbank or a neighbour's lot, or about a tree that has already fallen. Ask the Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office at 0919 081 4509.
