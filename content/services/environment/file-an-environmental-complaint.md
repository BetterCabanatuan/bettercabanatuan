# File an Environmental Complaint — Cabanatuan City

Residents and business owners in Cabanatuan City can file a complaint about an environmental problem, such as pollution, improper waste disposal, noise, smoke or a business violating a city environmental ordinance, by writing to the Office of the City Mayor. The Mayor's office endorses the letter to the Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CCENRO), which inspects within {TIME_INSPECTION}. Filing is {FEE_COMPLAINT}. This page gives the steps, what happens after the inspection, and the penalties a violator faces.

---

## Who may avail

Cabanatuan City residents and business owners with valid concerns or complaints. The charter classifies this as a **Simple** transaction (Government to Client and Government to Business).

## If a Notice of Violation is issued

A person or establishment caught violating a city environmental ordinance receives a Violator's Receipt or Notice of Violation (NoV) from CCENRO. The penalty is a fine or community service. To settle it, the violator brings the Violator's Receipt or NoV and the original and a photocopy of any valid ID to CCENRO.

### Fines

Fines are set by the Cabanatuan City Environment Code, Ordinance No. 052-2015. The violator submits the NoV, receives an Order of Payment, pays at the Office of the City Treasurer and presents the Official Receipt back to CCENRO. Total processing time: {TIME_FINE_TOTAL}.

### Community environmental service

No fee. The Enforcement and Regulatory Division Officer of the Day explains where the violator renders community service and for how many hours. The violator attends the scheduled service with the Task Force personnel ({TIME_COMMUNITY_SERVICE_MAX}), takes photos for documentation, and submits them to the ERD Officer, who issues a Certificate of Completion of Community Service signed by the Head of CCENRO. Total processing time: {TIME_COMMUNITY_SERVICE_TOTAL}.

## Illegal logging, quarrying or fishing

The charter has no separate entry for reporting illegal logging, quarrying or fishing. Use the complaint procedure above, or report to the PACD or the complaint text line. Quarrying in the city needs a Mayor's Clearance or Permit to Quarry from CCENRO; see [Get a hauling pass](/services/environment/get-a-hauling-pass) for the requirements.

The national Department of Environment and Natural Resources keeps its own Community Environment and Natural Resources Office in Cabanatuan, at the Capitol Compound, listed at (044) 940-9695, 411-1496 and 456-2234 in the [DENR Region 3 directory](https://r3.denr.gov.ph/index.php/about-us/denr-directory). It is a separate office from the city's CCENRO.

## Other complaint channels

If the city does not act on your complaint, you can escalate to the Anti-Red Tape Authority at complaints@arta.gov.ph or 8-478-5093, the Presidential Complaint Center at pcc@malacanang.gov.ph or 8888, or the Contact Center ng Bayan at email@contactcenterngbayan.gov.ph or 0908 881 6565.
