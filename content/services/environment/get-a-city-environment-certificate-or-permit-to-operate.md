# Get a City Environmental Certificate or Permit to Operate — Cabanatuan City

Businesses in Cabanatuan City get two environmental clearances from the Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CCENRO): the City Environmental Certificate (CEC) while the building is under construction, and the City Environmental Permit to Operate (CEPO) before the business opens. This page gives the checklist, the fee for each type of establishment, the steps and the processing time from the Citizen's Charter.

---

## Which one do you need

| Clearance                                   | When                                                                                               | Processing time   |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| City Environmental Certificate (CEC)        | During the construction stage of a business, commercial, institutional or industrial establishment | {TIME_CEC_TOTAL}  |
| City Environmental Permit to Operate (CEPO) | Before the establishment starts operating                                                          | {TIME_CEPO_TOTAL} |

Both are **Simple** transactions (Government to Business) for Cabanatuan City business establishments. The CEC is also on the checklist for a new Mayor's Business Permit; see [Apply for a barangay clearance and Mayor's business permit](/services/business/apply-for-barangay-clearance-and-mayors-business-permits).

## Steps

The steps are the same for the CEC and the CEPO. Only the total time differs.

| Step | What you do                                                                                  | What the city does                                                                                                    | Time                    | Who handles it                                    |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Submit the duly accomplished CCENRO Form 1 with complete requirements                        | Receives and examines the form and requirements                                                                       | {TIME_RECEIVE}          | Administrative personnel, Administrative Division |
|      |                                                                                              | Issues the Order of Payment                                                                                           | {TIME_ORDER_OF_PAYMENT} | Administrative personnel, Administrative Division |
| 2    | Pay at the Office of the City Treasurer and present the Official Receipt (OR) back to CCENRO | Records the OR number and releases the OR to you; advises you to wait for the ocular inspection by the CCENRO officer | {TIME_RECORD_OR}        | Administrative personnel, Administrative Division |
|      |                                                                                              | Conducts the inspection and evaluation                                                                                | {TIME_INSPECTION}       | Enforcement and Regulatory Division Officer       |
|      |                                                                                              | Prepares and signs the inspection report                                                                              | {TIME_REPORT}           | ERD Officer of the day; Head, CCENRO              |
|      |                                                                                              | Approves and signs the certification                                                                                  | {TIME_APPROVAL}         | Head, CCENRO; City Mayor                          |
|      |                                                                                              | Releases the certification to you                                                                                     | {TIME_RELEASE}          | Administrative personnel, Administrative Division |

**Total processing time: {TIME_CEC_TOTAL} for the CEC; {TIME_CEPO_TOTAL} for the CEPO.**

## Liquid Waste or Residual Waste Disposal Certificate

CCENRO also issues a Liquid Waste or Residual Waste Disposal Certificate to business establishments. The checklist is the same as above, filed on CCENRO Form 1. The fee is {FEE_LIQUID_WASTE}. The steps follow the same pattern, with the inspection and report handled by the Enforcement and Regulatory Division Officer or the Solid Waste Management Division (SWMD), and the certification approved by the Head of CCENRO. Total processing time: {TIME_LIQUID_WASTE_TOTAL}.

## What the charter does not cover

The charter does not state how long a CEC or CEPO stays valid, how to renew it, or how the office classifies a business that is not on the fee list. Ask the Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office at 0919 081 4509.
