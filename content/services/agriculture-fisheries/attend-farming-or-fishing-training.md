# Attend Farming or Fishing Training — Cabanatuan City

The Citizen's Charter lists no farming or fishing training service for the City Agriculture Office and publishes no training calendar, so ask the office directly what is scheduled. The one training the charter does name is a general livelihood skills training that individuals, associations and cooperatives can request from the City Livelihood and Cooperatives Development Office.

## Livelihood Skills / Employable Skills Training

The City Livelihood and Cooperatives Development Office provides livelihood skills and employable skills training for livelihood and job opportunities. The charter classifies the service as **Simple**. Individuals, associations, cooperatives and groups in Cabanatuan City may avail. The charter does not say which skills are taught; ask the office.

**City Livelihood and Cooperatives Development Office**
CLCDO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 089 9875
