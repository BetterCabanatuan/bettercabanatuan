# Ask for Free Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers, or Fingerlings — Cabanatuan City

The City Agriculture Office gives out vegetable seeds and seedlings, forest and fruit-bearing trees, ornamental and herbal plants, rice seeds and fertilizer vouchers from the Department of Agriculture, free soil testing, and tilapia fingerlings. Everything is free except fingerlings, which cost {FEE_FINGERLINGS_PER_BAG} per bag. This page lists who qualifies, what to bring and how long each request takes.

## Vegetable seeds, seedlings, trees and other planting materials

The office accommodates requests for vegetable seeds and seedlings, forest trees, fruit-bearing trees, ornamental plants and herbal plants from individuals, farmers, associations and institutions, including for tree-planting activities. The charter classifies the service as **Simple**. Any bona fide citizen of Cabanatuan City may avail.

## Soil test analysis

A soil test tells you which crop nutrients your soil already has, so you can adjust fertilizer to what the soil and crop need. The office receives your samples, analyses them, forwards them to the Provincial or Regional Soil Laboratory and gives you a fertilizer recommendation. The charter classifies the service as **Complex**. Farmers registered in the General Master List whose farm is within the city may avail.

## Tilapia fingerlings

The office disperses tilapia fingerlings to small farmers and fisherfolk as livelihood support. The city endorses your request to the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR), which supplies the fingerlings; you then receive them on a dispersal date the office sets. The charter classifies the service as **Simple**. Fisherfolk registered in the General Master List whose farm area is within the city may avail.

## Farm machinery and infrastructure projects for farmer groups

The charter has no service for an individual to borrow farm tools or equipment from the city. What it offers is technical assistance to **registered farmer associations or organizations, farmers' cooperatives and irrigators' associations** that hold an accreditation certificate from the Department of Agriculture, so the group can obtain farm machinery, equipment and infrastructure projects from the Department of Agriculture and other partner agencies. The charter classifies the service as **Complex**.

### Checklist of requirements

| Requirement                          | Where to secure                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Letter request                       | From the group                                                                                              |
| Letter of intent                     | From the group                                                                                              |
| Project proposal or board resolution | From the group                                                                                              |
| Deed of donation or lease contract   | From the group                                                                                              |
| Photocopy of the land title          | Registry of Deeds, Cabanatuan City                                                                          |
| Photocopy of the tax declaration     | Office of the City Assessor                                                                                 |
| Vicinity map                         | From the group                                                                                              |
| CDA, SEC or DOLE registration        | Cooperative Development Authority, Securities and Exchange Commission or Department of Labor and Employment |
| Accreditation certificate from DA    | Department of Agriculture, Regional Office III                                                              |

### What happens

1. Register in the Client's Logbook and submit the complete set of requirements. The City Agriculturist forwards the request to the Supervising Agriculturist.
2. The Supervising Agriculturist and the assigned technologist interview the group, validate the papers and inspect the site in the barangay ({TIME_MACHINERY_ASSESSMENT}).
3. The City Agriculturist and the City Mayor prepare and sign a certification and endorsement to the partner agency ({TIME_MACHINERY_CERTIFICATION}). The partner agency's processing time depends on the availability of the requested item.
4. The partner agency grants and releases the request ({TIME_MACHINERY_GRANT}). The city and the agency validate and monitor the project afterwards ({TIME_MACHINERY_MONITORING}).

**Fee: none. Total processing time at the city: {TIME_MACHINERY_TOTAL}**, not counting the partner agency's own processing.
