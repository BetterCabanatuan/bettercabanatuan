# Get Veterinary Services for Livestock — Cabanatuan City

The City Veterinary Office treats livestock free of charge, usually by sending the veterinarian to your farm, and issues the Veterinary Health Certificate you need to transport pigs, carabao, cattle or chicken. It also certifies the cause of death of livestock and poultry for insurance claims, responds to animal disease reports, and runs barangay deworming drives. Large cattle are documented and branded at the Office of the City Treasurer. This page lists what to bring and how long each service takes.

## Veterinary Health Certificate for transporting animals

You need this certificate to move live animals such as pigs, carabao, cattle and chicken, so disease does not spread to their destination. The charter classifies the service as **Simple** and opens it to residents and businesses with animals for travel or transport.

### Checklist of requirements

| Animal              | Requirement                        | Where to secure                                                                      |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Dogs, cats, monkeys | Rabies vaccination record          | Your private veterinarian, or the City Veterinary Office if it vaccinated the animal |
| Chicken, gamefowl   | NCD vaccination record and AI test | Your veterinarian                                                                    |
| Swine               | Vaccination history                | Your veterinarian                                                                    |

## Certificate of post-mortem examination for livestock and poultry

A necropsy establishes the probable cause of death or disease of livestock, which you may need for insurance claims and similar matters. The charter classifies the service as **Complex**. Farmers who are residents of Cabanatuan City and RSBSA-validated may avail. If you have not validated your RSBSA documents, see [Register as a farmer (RSBSA) and get a Farmer's ID](/services/agriculture-fisheries/register-as-a-farmer-rsbsa-and-get-a-farmer-id).

### Checklist of requirements

| Requirement                                                                                         | Where to secure                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Records of animal population in the barangay, or your certificate of animal ownership, if available | City Veterinary Office                            |
| RSBSA documents for validation (encouraged)                                                         | City Veterinary Office or City Agriculture Office |

### Steps

| Step | What you do                                                                                         | What the city does                                                                        | Time               | Who handles it                              |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Go to the City Veterinary Office at City Hall and have your QR code scanned                         | Checks the requirements and records                                                       | {TIME_PM_INTAKE}   | Veterinarian in charge                      |
| 2    | Wait at the office                                                                                  | Schedules the post-mortem examination as soon as possible                                 | {TIME_PM_SCHEDULE} | Veterinarian                                |
| 3    | On the scheduled date, guide the veterinarian to the animal and give any other information about it | Arrives at the site for the necropsy                                                      | {TIME_PM_TRAVEL}   | Veterinarian                                |
| 4    | Wait for the results; you will be notified by call, text or other means                             | Performs the necropsy and decides whether to collect samples for further diagnostic tests | {TIME_PM_NECROPSY} | Veterinarian                                |

## Report an animal disease or outbreak

Pet owners, livestock owners, concerned citizens and barangays can report disease to the City Veterinary Office by letter, text, call or message on its page. The charter classifies disease investigation and outbreak response as **Complex**.

- **Disease investigation and surveillance.** The office receives the report ({TIME_SURVEILLANCE_INTAKE}), checks manpower, the schedule and your barangay's animal health record ({TIME_SURVEILLANCE_SCHEDULE}), then visits for interviews and sample collection ({TIME_SURVEILLANCE_VISIT}). Samples go to the Department of Agriculture Regional Animal Disease Diagnostic Laboratory (RADDL); {TIME_SURVEILLANCE_LAB}. There is no fee for outbreak surveillance, but RADDL may charge for laboratory work. The office notifies you and the barangays involved when results are released.
- **Outbreak response.** A barangay requests assistance; the office checks medicines, manpower and schedule and decides on measures, then the response team goes to the barangay ({TIME_OUTBREAK_VISIT}), holds a dialogue with stakeholders ({TIME_OUTBREAK_DIALOGUE}) and carries out control activities ({TIME_OUTBREAK_CONTROL}). The barangay provides guides and the venue. No fee.

## Barangay deworming drives for large animals

A barangay can request free mass deworming of large animals and other veterinary activities by sending a letter of request to the City Veterinary Office. The office receives the letter ({TIME_DEWORMING_RECEIVE}), checks the stock of medicines and supplements, manpower and schedule ({TIME_DEWORMING_CHECK}), proposes a tentative date ({TIME_DEWORMING_TENTATIVE}) and fixes the schedule ({TIME_DEWORMING_SCHEDULE}). The barangay provides guides and the location. Ask your barangay hall to send the request.

## Meat inspection

Meat vendors who slaughter at the Cabanatuan City Abattoir, Barangay Aduas Sur, get a Meat Inspection Certificate from the veterinarian or deputized meat inspectors assigned there, after ante-mortem and post-mortem inspection. The charter prints no fee for it. Ask the City Veterinary Office at 0919 089 9906.
