# Get Free Anti-Rabies Vaccination for Pets — Cabanatuan City

The City Veterinary Office vaccinates dogs, cats and other pets against rabies free of charge all year round, and treats sick pets for free. Bring the animal and its vaccination card. This page also covers barangay mass vaccination drives, the health certificate you need to travel with a pet, and what happens if your pet is caught as a stray and how to get it back.

## Free anti-rabies vaccination

The city runs a whole-year programme of free anti-rabies vaccines for pet owners, because immunity at the animal level prevents loss of human lives. The charter classifies the service as **Simple**. All residents of Cabanatuan City may avail.

## Mass vaccination in your barangay

A barangay can ask the City Veterinary Office for free mass anti-rabies vaccination, deworming and other veterinary activities by sending a letter of request. The office receives the letter ({TIME_MASSVAX_RECEIVE}), checks vaccine stocks, manpower, the barangay's risk level and its previous mass vaccination record ({TIME_MASSVAX_CHECK}), proposes a tentative date ({TIME_MASSVAX_TENTATIVE}) and fixes the schedule ({TIME_MASSVAX_SCHEDULE}). The barangay provides guides and the venue. No fee. Ask your barangay hall to send the request.

## Travelling with your pet

Dogs, cats and monkeys need a Veterinary Health Certificate to travel. Bring the rabies vaccination record; animals without one are advised to be vaccinated first. The certificate costs {FEE_VHC}, paid at the Office of the City Treasurer. The full steps are on [Get veterinary services for livestock](/services/agriculture-fisheries/get-veterinary-services-for-livestock).

## If your pet is caught as a stray

The City Veterinary Office's Animal Shelter Operation Division catches and impounds stray animals. The charter describes the process as follows.

### How catching operations work

- The office serves a notice of the catching operation to each barangay captain {TIME_IMPOUND_NOTICE}, and the barangay informs residents so they can keep their animals in.
- Only animals found at large at the time of the operation are caught, with barangay officials and workers present.
- Owners can check which animals were caught in the barangay record book and the acknowledgement receipt issued by dog pound personnel, which shows the date, place, time and identity of the animals ({TIME_IMPOUND_CHECK_RECORDS}).
- A barangay that caught the animal itself may hold it for two days. If it is not redeemed, the city shelter retrieves it to complete a five-day impoundment.

### Getting your pet back

| Step | What you do                                                                                            | What the city does                                                                                               | Fee                                                                            | Time                   |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| 1    | Go to the animal shelter and identify your pet                                                         | Shows you the records and the animals for identification; you may also view the animals inside the facility      | None                                                                           | {TIME_IMPOUND_VERIFY}  |
| 2    | Fill up the shelter's statement and proof of ownership form and ask for the certificate of impoundment | Confirms you are the owner, issues the certificate and assesses the amount due under City Ordinance No. 016-2015 | None                                                                           | {TIME_IMPOUND_ASSESS}  |
| 3    | Pay at the Office of the City Treasurer and bring back the official receipt                            | Releases the animal                                                                                              | {FEE_IMPOUND_PER_ANIMAL} per animal plus {FEE_IMPOUND_PER_DAY} per day of stay | {TIME_IMPOUND_RELEASE} |

### After five days

Animals not redeemed within five days are offered for adoption or euthanised by a licensed veterinarian designated with the authority of the Bureau of Animal Industry, and buried at the facility.

### Adopting an impounded animal

Qualified and certified responsible pet owners may adopt. Bring a certificate of good health for the animal, issued by a licensed veterinarian of the City Veterinary Office, and a certificate of responsible pet owner from your barangay captain. Shelter staff show you the animals available ({TIME_ADOPT_VIEW}); you then sign the adoption contract, which the Special Operation Officer IV approves ({TIME_ADOPT_CONTRACT}). No fee.
