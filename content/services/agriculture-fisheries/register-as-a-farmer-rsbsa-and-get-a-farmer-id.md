# Register as a Farmer (RSBSA) and Get a Farmer's ID — Cabanatuan City

A Farmer's ID from the City Agriculture Office is the document most farm support in Cabanatuan City asks for. It is free and takes {TIME_FARMER_ID_TOTAL} if your name is already in the office's General Master List of farmers. This page covers the Farmer's ID, the Certificate of Bona Fide Farmer, and who to ask about the Registry System for Basic Sector in Agriculture (RSBSA), which decides who receives Department of Agriculture rice seeds and fertilizer vouchers.

**City Agriculture Office**
CALMO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 089 9925

The ID card itself is encoded and printed by the **Community Affairs Office**, 1st Floor, City Hall. Phone: 0919 081 3983.

---

## Why you need a Farmer's ID

The charter lists a Farmer's ID among the requirements for [planting materials, soil testing and tilapia fingerlings](/services/agriculture-fisheries/ask-for-free-seeds-seedlings-fertilizers-or-fingerlings). Department of Agriculture rice seeds, fertilizer vouchers and financial assistance go only to rice farmers enrolled in the RSBSA. The City Veterinary Office also encourages farmers to validate their RSBSA documents before asking for a [post-mortem certificate for livestock or poultry](/services/agriculture-fisheries/get-veterinary-services-for-livestock).

## Getting into the RSBSA and the General Master List

The charter does not describe how a farmer is added to the General Master List or enrolled in the RSBSA. It states only that the beneficiary list for rice seeds and fertilizer vouchers is based on the RSBSA enrolment provided by the Department of Agriculture, and that the office's Agricultural Technologist assigned to your area verifies names against the General Masterlist. If you are not yet registered, ask the City Agriculture Office at 0919 089 9925 or the Agricultural Technologist assigned to your barangay.
