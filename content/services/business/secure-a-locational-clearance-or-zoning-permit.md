# Secure a Locational Clearance or Zoning Permit — Cabanatuan City

Before you build or open a business on a site in Cabanatuan City, the City Planning and Development Office issues a locational clearance, also called a zoning permit. The Citizen's Charter lists {TIME_LC_COMPLEX_TOTAL} for residential projects and {TIME_LC_HIGHLY_TECHNICAL_TOTAL} for apartments, commercial, industrial, agricultural, institutional and special projects. This page gives the checklists, the steps, the fee basis, and the related site zoning certificate and appeal.

---

## Who may avail

Private or public parties with a project located in the city. The charter classifies residential projects as **Complex** and apartments, dormitories, commercial, industrial, agro-industrial, agricultural, institutional and special projects as **Highly Technical**.

## Where to apply

**City Planning and Development Office**
2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 3219

Fees are paid at the cashier of the **Office of the City Treasurer**, 1st Floor, City Hall. Phone: 0919 089 9849 or 044 464 7696.

A locational clearance is on the checklist for a new [Mayor's business permit](/services/business/apply-for-barangay-clearance-and-mayors-business-permits) and for a building permit; see [Request zoning, land use or building permits](/services/housing-land-use/request-zoning-land-use-or-building-permits).

## How the fee is set

The zoning permit fee is computed from the submitted requirements under the Revised Revenue Code (City Ordinance No. 038-2010) and varies by classification. The charter prints no amount.

## Site zoning certificate

A site zoning certificate states the zoning of a property. The charter classifies it as **Simple** and says it can be released the same day if the property is encoded in the city's GIS database. Total processing time: {TIME_SZC_TOTAL}. Fee: {FEE_SITE_ZONING_CERTIFICATE}.

| Copy of Location Plan with Vicinity Map                                                                                    | Licensed Geodetic Engineer   |

Steps: secure the form, submit the letter and requirements, submit the accomplished application, pay at the Office of the City Treasurer, then receive the certificate from the Zoning Officer.

## If your application is denied

You may appeal to the Local Zoning Board of Adjustment and Appeals (LZBAA), which may also allow exceptions, variances and deviations from the Zoning Ordinance when its specific terms and conditions are met. The charter classifies the appeal as **Highly Technical**, with a total of {TIME_LC_APPEAL_TOTAL}. The requirements are the residential checklist above, with the application form as an appeal letter and an affidavit of non-objection from owners of adjacent property. The LZBAA Secretariat receives the appeal, the Board meets and endorses it to the Sangguniang Panlungsod for a resolution, and the LZBAA Chairperson or City Mayor signs the clearance. The fee is computed as for a zoning permit.
