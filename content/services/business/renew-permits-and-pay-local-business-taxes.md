# Renew Permits and Pay Local Business Taxes — Cabanatuan City

Renew your Mayor's Business Permit at the City Business Licensing and Investment Promotion Office and pay the business tax at the Office of the City Treasurer. The Citizen's Charter lists {TIME_RENEWAL_TOTAL} for the renewal and {TIME_BUSINESS_TAX_TOTAL} for the tax payment. This page gives the payment schedule, the checklist, the steps and the offices involved, and what to bring to retire a business, move it or change its ownership.

---

## Who may avail

Any business with a permit in Cabanatuan City. The charter classifies the renewal as **Simple** (Government to Client). Business tax is paid by business owners whose business is located within the city.

## Where to go

**City Business Licensing and Investment Promotion Office** — application, assessment and release of the permit
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 089 9869

**Office of the City Treasurer** — business tax and fees, Business Tax and Other City Taxes, Fees and Charges Division
1st Floor, City Hall
Phone: 0919 089 9849 or 044 464 7696

## When to pay

The charter sets this schedule for business tax:

| Payment                | Due date                |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Annual, or 1st quarter | On or before January 20 |
| 2nd quarter            | On or before April 20   |
| 3rd quarter            | On or before July 20    |
| 4th quarter            | On or before October 20 |

Payment is due within twenty days of January or of each following quarter.

## Closing, moving or changing the ownership of a business

These four services are handled at the City Business Licensing and Investment Promotion Office, with the Order of Payment and payment at the Office of the City Treasurer. The charter classifies each as **Simple** and gives a total of {TIME_RETIREMENT} for each. Steps are the same in all four: submit the documents at the licensing office (10 minutes), receive the Order of Payment from the Local Treasury Operation Officer IV (10 minutes), then pay and claim the certificate (10 minutes).

### Retire a business

Fee: {FEE_RETIREMENT}. Total time: {TIME_RETIREMENT}. You receive a Certification of Retirement of Business.

| Requirement                                                                                                                            | Where to secure                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Business License Certificate (1 original)                                                                                              | Business owner                   |
| Affidavit of business closure (1 original)                                                                                             | Law office                       |
| Barangay Certification of Business Closure (1 original)                                                                                | Barangay Hall                    |
| Statement of gross sales or receipts, supported by BIR Form 1701 (single proprietorship) or BIR Form 1702 (partnership or corporation) | Bureau of Internal Revenue       |
| Cancellation of business trade name (1 original)                                                                                       | Department of Trade and Industry |
| For a corporation: Board Resolution or Secretary's Certificate on the closure (1 original)                                             | Corporate Secretary              |
| Business tax clearance (1 original)                                                                                                    | Office of the City Treasurer     |
| If a representative files: Authorization Letter (1 original), owner's ID (1 photocopy) and representative's ID (1 photocopy)           | Business owner                   |

The charter states that if the tax due at retirement is less than the tax due for the current year based on gross sales or receipts, the difference must be paid before the business is considered officially retired.

### Change the business location

Fee: {FEE_CHANGE_LOCATION}. Total time: {TIME_CHANGE_LOCATION}. You receive a Business Permit Certificate stating the new address. Sign the client log book first.

| Business Permit (1 original)                                                                                                 | Business owner  |
| If a representative files: Authorization Letter (1 original), owner's ID (1 photocopy) and representative's ID (1 photocopy) | Business owner  |

## What the charter does not cover

Penalties for late payment of business tax are not in the charter; ask the Office of the City Treasurer at 0919 089 9849.

Opening a new business instead? See [Apply for a barangay clearance and Mayor's business permit](/services/business/apply-for-barangay-clearance-and-mayors-business-permits). Each worker also needs an [occupational permit](/services/business/get-an-occupational-or-working-permit).
