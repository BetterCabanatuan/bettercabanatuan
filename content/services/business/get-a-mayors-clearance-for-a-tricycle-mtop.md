# Get a Mayor's Clearance for a Tricycle (MTOP) — Cabanatuan City

Tricycle operators applying for a Motorized Tricycle Operators Permit (MTOP) need a Mayor's Clearance from the Office of the City Mayor. The Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_MTOP_CLEARANCE_TOTAL} and no fee. This page gives the checklist and the steps, and says what the charter does not cover.

---

## Who may avail

Tricycle operators. The charter classifies the service as **Simple** (Government to Client). The clearance is issued by the City Mayor's Office, Administrative Division, to meet the requirements of the Community Affairs Office's Traffic Management Section for the MTOP.

## Where to apply

**Office of the City Mayor**, Administrative Division
2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 3749

## Checklist of requirements

| Requirement                                            | Where to secure                |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Photocopy of Voter's ID, or Certification from COMELEC | COMELEC                        |
| Police Clearance                                       | Cabanatuan City Police Station |
| Barangay Clearance                                     | Your barangay                  |
| Community Tax Certificate (cedula)                     | Office of the City Treasurer   |

Get the cedula first: see [Get a community tax certificate (cedula)](/services/taxes-and-certificates/get-a-community-tax-certificate-cedula).

## Steps

| Step | What you do                                | What the city does                                                      | Time                |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| 1    | Submit the requirements                    | Receives and examines the completeness and validity of the requirements | {TIME_MTOP_RECEIVE} |
|      |                                            | Prepares the Mayor's Clearance                                          | {TIME_MTOP_PREPARE} |
|      |                                            | Signs the document, by authority of the City Mayor                      | {TIME_MTOP_SIGN}    |
|      |                                            | Dry-seals the document                                                  | {TIME_MTOP_SEAL}    |
| 2    | Sign the logbook                           | Front desk releases the clearance                                       | {TIME_MTOP_RELEASE} |
| 3    | Receive your copy of the Mayor's Clearance |                                                                         | {TIME_MTOP_RECEIPT} |

**Total processing time: {TIME_MTOP_CLEARANCE_TOTAL}.** The charter lists no fee for this clearance.

---

## What the charter does not cover

The MTOP franchise steps, the franchise fee, the fare matrix and the list of tricycle operators and drivers associations are not in the published charter. Ask the **Community Affairs Office**, 1st Floor, City Hall, phone 0919 081 3983.
