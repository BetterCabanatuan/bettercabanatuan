# Apply for a Barangay Clearance and Mayor's Business Permit — Cabanatuan City

Every business operating in Cabanatuan City needs a Mayor's Business Permit from the City Business Licensing and Investment Promotion Office. The barangay business clearance is one of the documents on its checklist. This page gives the office to visit and the national registrations you complete before applying.

---

## Where to apply

**City Business Licensing and Investment Promotion Office**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 089 9869

The Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of **2 days** for a new business permit. The full checklist of requirements, steps and fees from the charter will be published on this page.

---

## Before you apply: national registrations

These are completed with national agencies, not the city.

1. **Register the business name or entity.**
   - Sole proprietors register the business name with [DTI](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/).
   - Partnerships and corporations register with [SEC eSPARC](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/).
   - Cooperatives register with the [Cooperative Development Authority](https://cda.gov.ph/).
2. **Register with the BIR** at the Revenue District Office covering your business address, using BIR Form 1901 (sole proprietor) or 1903 (partnership or corporation). See [bir.gov.ph](https://www.bir.gov.ph/).
3. **Register as an employer** if you will hire staff: [SSS](https://www.sss.gov.ph/), [PhilHealth](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/), [Pag-IBIG](https://www.pagibigfund.gov.ph/).

---

## Documents the charter checklist names

The new business permit checklist in the Citizen's Charter includes the DTI, SEC or CDA registration, a lease contract or proof of ownership, the barangay business clearance, a locational clearance from the City Planning and Development Office, a sanitary permit from the City Health Office, building and occupancy permits from the Office of the City Engineer, a City Environment Certificate, a Fire Safety Inspection Certificate from the BFP, and SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG clearances. Bring the originals and photocopies.
