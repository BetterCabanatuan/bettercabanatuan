# Apply for a Barangay Clearance and Mayor's Business Permit — Cabanatuan City

Every business in Cabanatuan City needs a Mayor's Business Permit from the City Business Licensing and Investment Promotion Office. The Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_NEW_PERMIT} for a new permit. This page gives the full checklist, the steps, the offices involved and how the fee is set.

---

## Who may avail

Anyone opening a business in Cabanatuan City. The charter classifies the service as **Simple** (Government to Client).

## Where to apply

**City Business Licensing and Investment Promotion Office**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 089 9869

---

## Before you apply: national registrations

These are completed with national agencies, not the city. The charter asks for proof of registration as the first item on its checklist.

- Sole proprietors register the business name with [DTI](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/).
- Partnerships and corporations register with [SEC](https://www.sec.gov.ph/).
- Cooperatives register with the [Cooperative Development Authority](https://cda.gov.ph/).
- If you will hire staff, register as an employer with [SSS](https://www.sss.gov.ph/), [PhilHealth](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/) and [Pag-IBIG](https://www.pagibigfund.gov.ph/). Their clearances are on the city's checklist.

### About the barangay business clearance

The charter lists the Barangay Hall as the place to secure it. The project research note records that DILG Memorandum Circular 2019-177 directs cities to receive and issue the barangay clearance for business permits at the city's business one-stop shop, under section 11(f) of the Ease of Doing Business Act, [RA 11032](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2018/ra_11032_2018.html) ([circular summary](https://batasnatin.com/laws/mc-2019-177)). Ask the office which arrangement applies when you file.

## How the fee is set

There is no fixed fee. The city assesses business tax, regulatory fees and charges in one Order of Payment under the Revised Revenue Code (City Ordinance No. 038-2010), together with the Bureau of Fire Protection fees. You pay once at the Office of the City Treasurer.

One item in the backroom assessment has a stated fee: the City Planning and Development Office approves the business permit application for locational clearance at {FEE_CPDO_BUSINESS_PERMIT_APPROVAL} per business permit, in {TIME_CPDO_BUSINESS_PERMIT_APPROVAL}.

Business tax payment is explained on [Renew permits and pay local business taxes](/services/business/renew-permits-and-pay-local-business-taxes). Your workers each need an [occupational permit](/services/business/get-an-occupational-or-working-permit), which requires a [community tax certificate (cedula)](/services/taxes-and-certificates/get-a-community-tax-certificate-cedula).
