# Get an Occupational or Working Permit — Cabanatuan City

Workers and employees in Cabanatuan City get an occupational permit from the City Business Licensing and Investment Promotion Office. The Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_OCCUPATIONAL_TOTAL} and a fee that depends on the type of establishment you work in. This page gives the checklist, the steps and the fees.

---

## Who may avail

Anyone. The charter classifies the service as **Simple** (Government to Client).

## Where to apply

**City Business Licensing and Investment Promotion Office**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 089 9869

Fees are paid at the **Office of the City Treasurer**, 1st Floor, City Hall. Phone: 0919 089 9849 or 044 464 7696.

---

## Checklist of requirements

| Requirement                                       | Where to secure                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PSA Birth Certificate or any government-issued ID | PSA or the City Civil Registry Office                          |
| Police or NBI Clearance                           | Philippine National Police or National Bureau of Investigation |
| Community Tax Certificate (cedula)                | Office of the City Treasurer                                   |
| Notarized parent consent, if you are a minor      | Your parent                                                    |

Get the cedula first: see [Get a community tax certificate (cedula)](/services/taxes-and-certificates/get-a-community-tax-certificate-cedula).

## Fees

The fee varies by type of occupation, as printed in the charter.

| Where you work                                                                        | Fee                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Establishments generally considered "Offensive and Dangerous Business Establishments" | {FEE_OCCUPATIONAL_OFFENSIVE_DANGEROUS} |
| Commercial establishments that cater to the daily needs of the general public         | {FEE_OCCUPATIONAL_COMMERCIAL}          |
| Food or eatery establishments                                                         | {FEE_OCCUPATIONAL_FOOD}                |
| Night, or night and day, establishments                                               | {FEE_OCCUPATIONAL_NIGHT}               |

The charter does not say which trades count as offensive and dangerous. Ask the office at 0919 089 9869.

Your employer's permit is a separate transaction: see [Apply for a barangay clearance and Mayor's business permit](/services/business/apply-for-barangay-clearance-and-mayors-business-permits).
