# Rent Stalls in Public Markets — Cabanatuan City

Public markets in Cabanatuan City are managed by the City Economic Enterprise and Public Utilities Management Office (CEEPUMO). Stallholders deal with this office for market clearances and certificates.

---

## Where to go

**City Economic Enterprise and Public Utilities Management Office**
CEEMPUMO Bldg., Sanciangco St., Barangay Supermarket, Cabanatuan City
Phone: 0919 081 2785
Email: ceepumocab@gmail.com

---

## What the Citizen's Charter covers

The charter lists two stallholder services at this office:

| Service            | Fee                      | Total processing time     |

| Market certificate | {FEE_MARKET_CERTIFICATE} | {TIME_MARKET_CERTIFICATE} |

A market clearance is also on the checklist for a new business permit when the business is a market stall.

**Stall awards and rentals are not in the published charter.** Ask the office directly about vacant stalls, rental rates and the award process.
