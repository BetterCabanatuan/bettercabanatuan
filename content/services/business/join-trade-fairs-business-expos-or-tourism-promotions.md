# Join Trade Fairs, Business Expos, or Tourism Promotions — Cabanatuan City

The City Information and Tourism Office handles tourism promotion for Cabanatuan City. Trade fairs and expos are announced by the city offices below as they are scheduled. There is no fixed calendar published in the Citizen's Charter.

---

## Who to ask

- **City Information and Tourism Office** — tourism promotion activities and city events.
  2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100. Phone: 0919 089 9862.
- **City Business Licensing and Investment Promotion Office** — investment promotion and business registration.
  1st Floor, City Hall. Phone: 0919 089 9869.
- **Public Employment Service Office (PESO)** — job fairs and livelihood events.
  PESO Bldg., City Hall Compound. Phone: 0919 081 1300. Announcements: [facebook.com/PESOCabanatuan](https://www.facebook.com/PESOCabanatuan/).
- **City Livelihood and Cooperatives Development Office** — cooperative and livelihood group participation.
  CLCDO Bldg., City Hall Compound. Phone: 0919 089 9875.

---

## What organisers usually ask for

Requirements differ per event. Have these ready so you can register quickly when a call is announced:

- Your current Mayor's Business Permit and DTI, SEC or CDA registration
- A product or service list with prices and photos
- Product compliance documents where they apply, such as FDA registration for food and cosmetics
- Booth needs: table, power, display space
