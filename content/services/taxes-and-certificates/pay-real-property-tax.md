# Pay Real Property Tax — Cabanatuan City

If you own land, a building or machinery in Cabanatuan City, you pay real property tax (RPT) every year at the Office of the City Treasurer. Pay the whole year on or before 1 January, or pay in four quarterly instalments. You get a {DISCOUNT_ON_TIME} discount for paying on time, a {DISCOUNT_ADVANCE} discount for paying before the year starts, and a {PENALTY_MONTHLY} charge on anything paid late. The Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_RPT_TOTAL} at the counter.

---

## Where to go

**Office of the City Treasurer**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 089 9849; 044 464 7696

Start at the Real Property Tax Division, then pay at the Cash Receipts Division window assigned to real property tax.

---

## Who pays

Any individual or juridical person who owns real property within Cabanatuan City. The charter describes RPT as a yearly ad valorem tax on real property such as land, machinery and improvements not specifically exempted under the law, citing section 232 of the Local Government Code. The charter classifies the service as **Simple** (Government to Client).

---

## When to pay

Pay for the whole year on 1 January, or in four equal instalments:

| Quarter     | Pay between               |
| ----------- | ------------------------- |
| 1st quarter | 1 January and 31 March    |
| 2nd quarter | 1 April and 30 June       |
| 3rd quarter | 1 July and 30 September   |
| 4th quarter | 1 October and 31 December |

### Discounts and penalties

| If you pay                                             | What happens                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| On or before the deadline                              | {DISCOUNT_ON_TIME} discount                                       |
| Before 1 January of the taxable year (advance payment) | {DISCOUNT_ADVANCE} discount                                       |
| After the scheduled date                               | {PENALTY_MONTHLY} interest on the unpaid amount or fraction of it |

The charter notes that the penalty only starts in the second quarter of the current year.

## How the tax is computed

The charter gives two components, each based on the assessed value of the property:

| Component                    | Formula in the charter                                                           |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Basic tax                    | (Assessed value × {RATE_BASIC}) − (Basic tax × discount) + (Basic tax × penalty) |
| Special Education Fund (SEF) | (Assessed value × {RATE_SEF}) − (SEF tax × discount) + (SEF tax × penalty)       |

The Order of Payment shows the final amount. Keep the Official Receipt; you need it to [get a real property tax clearance](/services/taxes-and-certificates/get-a-real-property-tax-clearance).

## National context

Real property taxation by cities is governed by Title II, Book II of the Local Government Code of 1991 (Republic Act No. 7160). Read the law at [lawphil.net](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html).
