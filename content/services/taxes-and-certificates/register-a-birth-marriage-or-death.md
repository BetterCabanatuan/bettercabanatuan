# Register a Birth, Marriage or Death — Cabanatuan City

Births, marriages and deaths that happen in Cabanatuan City are recorded at the City Civil Registry Office. Registration on time is {FEE_TIMELY} and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_TIMELY_TOTAL} for each. Register a birth within 30 days, a marriage within 15 days of the wedding, and a death within 30 days. Miss the deadline and the record goes through late registration instead, which carries a fee and a posting period.

---

## Where to go

**City Civil Registry Office**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 4548; 044 456 6920

The charter classifies all three services as **Simple** (Government to Client).

## Register a birth

Register within **30 days** of the birth at the civil registry of the place where the birth occurred.

### What to bring

| Requirement                                                                                                                                                                           | Where to secure                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Four copies of the Certificate of Live Birth (Form 102), duly accomplished and signed by the proper parties                                                                           | Hospital, clinic or attendant; City Civil Registry Office |
| Medical certificate from the hospital, clinic or midwife, or an Affidavit of Traditional Midwife ("Hilot"); if the attendant has died, an affidavit of the mother, father or guardian | Hospital, clinic, midwife or affiant                      |
| Certified true copy of the parents' marriage contract                                                                                                                                 | City Civil Registry Office                                |
| If the child is illegitimate: Authority to Use the Surname of the Father, if applicable                                                                                               | Father                                                    |

### Steps

| Step | What you do                                       | What the city does                                               | Fee  | Time               | Who handles it       |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---- | ------------------ | -------------------- |
| 1    | Submit the accomplished Certificate of Live Birth | Receives and reviews it                                          | None | {TIME_TIMELY_STEP} | Registration Officer |
|      |                                                   | Signs the certificate and registers it in the registry of births |      | {TIME_TIMELY_STEP} | Registration Officer |
| 2    | Claim the document                                | Releases the owner's copy of the registered certificate          |      | {TIME_TIMELY_STEP} | Registration Officer |

**Total processing time: {TIME_TIMELY_TOTAL}.**

If the 30 days have passed, see [Register a birth late (delayed registration)](/services/taxes-and-certificates/register-a-birth-late-delayed-registration).

## Register a marriage

Submit the Certificate of Marriage within **15 days** of the wedding. For marriages exempt from the licence requirement, the period is **30 days**. The service covers all marriages that take place in Cabanatuan City.

### What to bring

| Requirement                                | Where to secure     |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------- |
| Four copies of the Certificate of Marriage | Solemnizing officer |

### Steps

| Step | What you do                                     | What the city does                                                | Fee  | Time               | Who handles it          |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---- | ------------------ | ----------------------- |
| 1    | Submit the four copies of the marriage contract | Reviews the copies                                                | None | {TIME_TIMELY_STEP} | Registration Officer II |
|      |                                                 | Registers the contract and assigns a registry number              |      | {TIME_TIMELY_STEP} | Registration Officer II |
| 2    | Receive your copy                               | Distributes the registered copy bearing the civil registry number |      | {TIME_TIMELY_STEP} | Registration Officer II |

**Total processing time: {TIME_TIMELY_TOTAL}.**

### Marriage without a licence under Article 34 of the Family Code

The charter quotes Article 34: no licence is needed for the marriage of a man and a woman who have lived together as husband and wife for at least five years and have no legal impediment to marry each other. The couple submits an Affidavit of Joint Cohabitation for evaluation and receives the registered marriage certificate. The fee is {FEE_ART34_LATE} if late, and the total processing time is {TIME_ART34_TOTAL}.

To get a licence before the wedding, see [Apply for a marriage license](/services/taxes-and-certificates/apply-for-a-marriage-license).

## Register a death

Register within the **30-day** reglementary period. The service covers all deaths that occur in Cabanatuan City.

### What to bring

| Requirement                                                      | Where to secure                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Four duly accomplished copies of Form 103 (Certificate of Death) | Hospital or attending physician; City Civil Registry Office |

### Steps

| Step | What you do                      | What the city does                                       | Fee  | Time               | Who handles it       |
| ---- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---- | ------------------ | -------------------- |
| 1    | Present the Certificate of Death | Receives and reviews the document                        | None | {TIME_TIMELY_STEP} | Registration Officer |
|      |                                  | Signs the certificate and enters it in the Registry Book |      | {TIME_TIMELY_STEP} | Registration Officer |
| 2    | Claim the document               | Releases the owner's copy                                |      | {TIME_TIMELY_STEP} | Registration Officer |

**Total processing time: {TIME_TIMELY_TOTAL}.**

## After registering

Certified copies of the registered record are issued by the same office. See [Request a birth, marriage or death certificate](/services/taxes-and-certificates/request-a-birth-marriage-or-death-certificate). To fix a typographical error in the record, see [Correct a clerical error in a civil registry record](/services/taxes-and-certificates/correct-a-clerical-error-in-a-civil-registry-record).
