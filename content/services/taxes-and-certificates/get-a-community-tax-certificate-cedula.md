# Get a Community Tax Certificate (Cedula) — Cabanatuan City

A Community Tax Certificate, or cedula, is the basic document the city issues to identify you and your residence for legal transactions. You get it at the Cash Receipts Division of the Office of the City Treasurer. The fee depends on whether you are employed, unemployed or a corporation, and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_CTC_TOTAL}.

---

## Where to go

**Office of the City Treasurer**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 089 9849; 044 464 7696

Go to the Cash Receipts Division.

---

## Who can get one

- Every inhabitant of the Philippines aged 18 or over who has been regularly employed on a wage or salary for at least 30 consecutive working days during the calendar year, or who is engaged in business or an occupation.
- Every corporation, however created or organised, domestic or resident foreign, engaged in or doing business in the Philippines.

The charter classifies the service as **Simple** (Government to Client).

---

## What to bring

| Requirement                                                                                          | Where to secure                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Completed Community Tax Certificate information sheet                                                | Office of the City Treasurer, Cash Receipts Division |
| Previous Community Tax Certificate, if available                                                     | You                                                  |
| Any ID that confirms your identity and residence (Voter's ID, driver's license, UMID card or others) | You                                                  |

**For a corporation**, also bring a document showing the total gross receipts of the preceding year.

## National context

The community tax is set by sections 156 to 164 of the Local Government Code of 1991 (Republic Act No. 7160). Read the law at [lawphil.net](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html).
