# Correct a Clerical Error in a Civil Registry Record — Cabanatuan City

A misspelt name, a wrong letter or a typing mistake in a birth, marriage or death record can be fixed by petition at the City Civil Registry Office under Republic Act No. 9048, without going to court. The filing fee is {FEE_RA9048_FILING} plus a {FEE_RA9048_INDORSEMENT} indorsement fee, {FEE_RA9048_TOTAL} in total. The petition is posted for 10 days, decided within 5 working days after that, and then sent to the Philippine Statistics Authority for affirmation, so expect to follow up {TIME_RA9048_FOLLOWUP}.

**City Civil Registry Office**

Fees are paid at the **Office of the City Treasurer**, also on the 1st Floor (0919 089 9849; 044 464 7696).

---

## Who can file

The owner of the record that contains the error, or the owner's spouse, children, parent, brother, sister, grandparents, guardian, or any other person authorised by law or by the owner. An authorised person files with a Special Power of Attorney (SPA). The charter classifies the service as **Highly Technical** (Government to Client).

The charter describes the City Civil Registrar's authority under RA 9048 as correcting clerical or typographical errors and changing a first name or nickname in the civil register.

| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Certified true machine copy of the certificate, or of the page of the Registry Book containing the entry to be corrected, and the PSA copy on security paper (SECPA) | City Civil Registry Office; Philippine Statistics Authority |
| At least **two** public or private documents showing the correct entry                                                                                               | See list below                                              |
| Other relevant documents the petitioner or the City Civil Registrar may consider necessary for approval                                                              |                                                             |

Documents the charter lists as evidence of the correct entry:

- Baptismal certificate
- Birth certificate (of the wife or husband, for a marriage petition)
- Voter's affidavit or registration record
- GSIS or SSS record
- Medical record
- Business record
- School records

## Other changes to a record

The charter lists a separate petition for a change of first name under RA 9048, and for correction of the day and month of birth or of sex under Republic Act No. 10172. Those petitions have their own requirements and fees; ask the City Civil Registry Office before filing. Errors that are not clerical, such as a change of surname or of the year of birth, are outside RA 9048 and need a court order.

---

## National context

- Republic Act No. 9048 (2001) authorises the city civil registrar to correct clerical or typographical errors and to change a first name or nickname without a judicial order. Read it at [lawphil.net](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2001/ra_9048_2001.html).
- Republic Act No. 10172 (2012) extends that authority to the day and month of birth and to sex. Read it at [lawphil.net](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2012/ra_10172_2012.html).
