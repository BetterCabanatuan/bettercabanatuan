# Get a Real Property Tax Clearance — Cabanatuan City

A real property tax clearance proves that the tax on your property is fully paid, with no delinquencies or unpaid obligations. You get it from the Office of the City Treasurer for a {FEE_RPT_CLEARANCE} certification fee, and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_RPT_CLEARANCE_TOTAL}. You will usually need it when selling or transferring property.

---

## Where to go

**Office of the City Treasurer**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 089 9849; 044 464 7696

Start at the Administrative Section, then pay at the Cash Receipts Division window for other fees and charges.

---

## Who can apply

Any taxpayer, resident or non-resident, who owns real property located within Cabanatuan City. The charter describes the clearance as an official document issued by the City Treasurer or a deputy declaring that the property owner or person with legal interest has updated payments on the property, with no record of delinquencies or unpaid tax obligations. The charter classifies the service as **Simple** (Government to Client).

---

## What to bring

| Requirement                                                                                                             | Where to secure                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Latest Official Receipt of the real property tax paid                                                                   | Issued by the Office of the City Treasurer           |
| Copy of the tax declaration or title, with the verified latest Official Receipt number of the real property tax payment | Office of the City Assessor                          |
| Official Receipt for the tax clearance fee (issued during step 2 below)                                                 | Office of the City Treasurer, Cash Receipts Division |

Your tax must be up to date before you apply. See [Pay real property tax](/services/taxes-and-certificates/pay-real-property-tax).
