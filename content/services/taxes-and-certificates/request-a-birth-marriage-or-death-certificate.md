# Request a Birth, Marriage or Death Certificate — Cabanatuan City

The City Civil Registry Office issues certified copies of birth, marriage and death records registered in Cabanatuan City. The fee is {FEE_CRD_COPY} and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_CRD_TOTAL}. The owner of the record, close family and a few other authorised people can request a copy; each brings a different proof of their right to it.

---

## Where to go

**City Civil Registry Office**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 4548; 044 456 6920

The fee is paid at the **Office of the City Treasurer**, also on the 1st Floor (0919 089 9849; 044 464 7696).

This is the city's copy of the record. Copies on PSA security paper are issued by the [Philippine Statistics Authority](https://psa.gov.ph/).

---

## Who can request a copy

The owner of the document, or the owner's spouse, children, parents, brothers, sisters, grandparents or guardian, or any other person authorised by law or by the owner. The charter classifies the service as **Simple** (Government to Client).

---

## What to bring

Everyone fills in the application form. Add the proof for your relationship to the record:

| Who is requesting                                                                                                                               | What to bring                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The document owner                                                                                                                              | Valid ID                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Spouse (for the record of the husband or wife and their children)                                                                               | Application form; valid government-issued ID                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Parent (for a child's record, if named as father or mother on it)                                                                               | Application form; valid government-issued ID of the document owner if of legal age, or of the parents if a minor                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Guardian appointed by a court, or a person exercising substitute parental authority under Article 216 of the Family Code (for a minor's record) | Application form; valid government-issued ID; court decree of guardianship, as applicable                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Institution legally in charge of a minor                                                                                                        | Application form; DSWD authorisation letter for that child, issued by the DSWD Regional Director to the Child Caring Agency                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Court or proper public official, for administrative, judicial or other official proceedings                                                     | Application form; Subpoena Duces Tecum and Ad Testificandum                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Government agency, in pursuit of its mandate                                                                                                    | Application form; Data Sharing Agreement under NPC Circular 16-02                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Nearest kin of a deceased person                                                                                                                | Application form; Affidavit of Kinship stating that you are the closest surviving relative                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Another person authorised by the owner or by a person entitled to the record                                                                    | Application form; written authorisation from the owner (or parents if a minor) together with one government-issued ID of the owner and one of the representative. A scanned image of the signed authorisation is acceptable if the signature matches the ID. If the owner is abroad, add a copy of their passport |
