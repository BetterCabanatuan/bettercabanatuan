# Transfer a Tax Declaration to a New Owner — Cabanatuan City

After you buy, inherit or receive real property in Cabanatuan City, the tax declaration must be moved into your name at the Office of the City Assessor so that future real property tax is billed to you. The same service covers subdivision and consolidation of lots. The certification fee is {FEE_TRANSFER_TD} and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_TRANSFER_TD_TOTAL}, after the transfer tax has been paid at the Office of the City Treasurer.

---

## Where to go

**Office of the City Assessor**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 089 9867

Fees and the transfer tax are paid at the **Office of the City Treasurer**, also on the 1st Floor (0919 089 9849; 044 464 7696).

---

## Who can apply

Any person, natural or juridical, who owns real property within Cabanatuan City. The charter classifies the service as **Simple** (Government to Client).

---

## Before you come: pay the transfer tax

The Office of the City Assessor asks for the transfer tax certificate or Official Receipt, so pay the transfer tax first at the Business Tax and Other City Taxes, Fees and Charges Division of the Office of the City Treasurer. The charter lists:

| Item                  | Charter entry                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Transfer tax          | {FEE_TRANSFER_TAX}                                                                                                                                                               |
| Certification fee     | {FEE_TRANSFER_TAX_CERT}                                                                                                                                                          |
| Documents to bring    | Tax declaration (Office of the City Assessor); Transfer Certificate of Title; notarised Deed of Sale or Donation; Official Receipt for the transfer tax (Cash Receipts Division) |
| Total processing time | {TIME_TRANSFER_TAX_TOTAL}                                                                                                                                                        |

You pay, receive the Official Receipt, then get the Certificate of Transfer Tax from the Administrative Section.

## What to bring to the Office of the City Assessor

| Requirement                                                                                                                                                              | Where to secure              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Photocopy of the Transfer Certificate of Title (1 copy)                                                                                                                  | Register of Deeds            |
| Photocopy of the Deed of Sale, Deed of Donation, Extrajudicial Settlement or any other document showing the transfer of the property from one person to another (1 copy) | Transferee                   |
| Certificate Authorizing Registration (CAR), original and 1 photocopy                                                                                                     | Bureau of Internal Revenue   |
| Transfer tax certificate or Official Receipt                                                                                                                             | Office of the City Treasurer |
| Updated tax payment (original or photocopy) or tax clearance                                                                                                             | Office of the City Treasurer |
| Certification fee                                                                                                                                                        | Office of the City Treasurer |

### Additional requirements by case

| Case                                                                                 | Add                                                                                                                       | Where to secure                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Bank-foreclosed or merged property                                                   | Certificate of Sale or Affidavit of Consolidation of Ownership; Board Resolution; SEC Certificate                         | Bank concerned                      |
| Subdivision or consolidation                                                         | Approved subdivision or consolidation plan                                                                                | Register of Deeds / LRA / surveyor  |
| Land awarded to farmer beneficiaries under the Comprehensive Agrarian Reform Program | Photocopy of the Certificate of Land Ownership Award (CLOA) or Emancipation Patent (EP); updated tax payment              | Owner; Office of the City Treasurer |
| Filed by a representative                                                            | Photocopy of the valid ID of the representative and of the owner; authorization letter or Special Power of Attorney (SPA) | You                                 |

See [Get a real property tax clearance](/services/taxes-and-certificates/get-a-real-property-tax-clearance) for the tax clearance.
