# Apply for a Marriage License — Cabanatuan City

Before you marry, you and your partner each file a sworn application for a marriage licence at the City Civil Registry Office of the city or municipality where either of you lives. The fees total {FEE_ML_TOTAL}, the application is posted for {TIME_ML_POSTING}, and the Citizen's Charter lists a processing time of {TIME_ML_TOTAL}. The licence is valid anywhere in the Philippines for {VALIDITY_ML} from the date of issue.

---

## Where to go

**City Civil Registry Office**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 4548; 044 456 6920

Fees are paid at the **Office of the City Treasurer**, also on the 1st Floor (0919 089 9849; 044 464 7696).

---

## Who can apply

Couples who wish to marry and of whom at least one lives in Cabanatuan City. Both must appear in person. The charter classifies the service as **Simple** (Government to Client).

---

## What to bring

### Both applicants

| Requirement                                                         | Where to secure                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Personal appearance of both parties                                 |                                                                      |
| Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR)                                | Philippine Statistics Authority                                      |
| Birth certificate or baptismal certificate (photocopy)              | Philippine Statistics Authority; church, for a baptismal certificate |
| Valid ID (photocopy)                                                | You                                                                  |
| 2x2 picture (1 piece)                                               | You                                                                  |
| Family Planning Seminar or Pre-Marriage Counselling Seminar         | POPCOM; DSWD                                                         |
| Parental Advice (ages 22 to 25) or Parental Consent (ages 18 to 21) | Parents                                                              |
| Barangay residency certificate, or an ID showing your residence     | Barangay                                                             |

### Additional requirements by case

| Case                   | Add                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Previously married     | Divorce decree, or judicial decree of annulment or nullity of marriage                                                                            |
| Widow or widower       | Copy of the Certificate of Death of the former spouse                                                                                             |
| Not a Filipino citizen | Original Certificate of Legal Capacity to Marry issued by your embassy; photocopy of your passport showing your latest arrival in the Philippines |

## After the wedding

The solemnizing officer's Certificate of Marriage must be registered within 15 days of the ceremony. See [Register a birth, marriage or death](/services/taxes-and-certificates/register-a-birth-marriage-or-death). Couples who have lived together for at least five years without legal impediment may marry without a licence under Article 34 of the Family Code; the same page explains what to submit.
