# Request a Certified True Copy of a Tax Declaration — Cabanatuan City

A tax declaration is the Office of the City Assessor's record of your property and its assessed value. You need a certified true copy when paying real property tax, applying for a tax clearance, selling or transferring property, or dealing with a bank. The certification fee is {FEE_CTC_TD} and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_CTC_TD_TOTAL}.

---

## Where to go

**Office of the City Assessor**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 089 9867

The certification fee is paid at the **Office of the City Treasurer**, also on the 1st Floor (0919 089 9849; 044 464 7696).

---

## Who can apply

Any person, natural or juridical, who owns real property within Cabanatuan City. The charter classifies the service as **Simple** (Government to Client).

---

## What to bring

| Requirement                                       | Where to secure             |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Duly accomplished request form or written request | Office of the City Assessor |
| Valid ID                                          | You                         |

If a representative applies for you, add:

- Photocopy of the valid ID of the owner and of the representative
- Authorization letter or Special Power of Attorney (SPA)
