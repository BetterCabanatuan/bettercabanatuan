# Register a Birth Late (Delayed Registration) — Cabanatuan City

A birth not registered within 30 days goes through delayed registration at the City Civil Registry Office. The fee is {FEE_LATE_BIRTH} ({FEE_LATE_BIRTH_TOTAL_NOT_MARRIED} if the parents are not married), the application is posted for {TIME_LATE_BIRTH_POSTING}, and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_LATE_BIRTH_TOTAL}. The requirements differ for an adult registering their own birth and for a child.

---

## Where to go

**City Civil Registry Office**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 4548; 044 456 6920

Fees are paid at the **Office of the City Treasurer**, also on the 1st Floor (0919 089 9849; 044 464 7696).

---

## Who can apply

Anyone who needs to register a live birth that was not registered within the 30-day period of regular registration. The charter classifies the service as **Simple** (Government to Client).

---

## What to bring

### If the person being registered is an adult

| Requirement                                                                                                                                                   | Where to secure                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| PSA Negative Record (certificate that no birth record exists)                                                                                                 | Philippine Statistics Authority      |
| National Certification from the National Archives, if born in 1944 or earlier                                                                                 | National Archives of the Philippines |
| Affidavit of two disinterested persons                                                                                                                        | Affiants                             |
| Any **two** documents that show the name of the child, date and place of birth, and name of the mother (and of the father if the child has been acknowledged) | See list below                       |

Documents the charter accepts as evidence:

- Baptismal certificate
- School records (nursery, kindergarten or preparatory)
- Income tax return of the parent or parents
- Insurance policy
- Medical records
- Others such as a Barangay Captain's certification
- Other authentic documents showing name, date and place of birth, such as a marriage contract, voter's registration or SSS record

### If the person being registered is a child

| Requirement                                                        | Where to secure                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| PSA Negative Record                                                | Philippine Statistics Authority |
| Personal appearance of the mother and father                       |                                 |
| Personal appearance of the hilot or midwife who attended the birth |                                 |
| Valid ID of the mother and father                                  |                                 |

## Steps

| Step | What you do                                              | What the city does                                   | Fee                                                                                                  | Time                       | Who handles it               |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 1    | Submit the documents for evaluation                      | Receives and evaluates the documents                 | None                                                                                                 | {TIME_LATE_BIRTH_EVALUATE} | Admin Aide III               |
| 2    | Pay the assessed fee at the Office of the City Treasurer | Receives the payment and issues the Official Receipt | {FEE_LATE_BIRTH} late registration; {FEE_LATE_BIRTH_NOT_MARRIED} more if the parents are not married | {TIME_LATE_BIRTH_PAY}      | Office of the City Treasurer |
| 3    | Wait for the posting period                              | Posts the application                                | None                                                                                                 | {TIME_LATE_BIRTH_POSTING}  | Admin Aide III               |
| 4    | Receive the registered birth certificate                 | Releases the registered certificate                  | None                                                                                                 | {TIME_LATE_BIRTH_RELEASE}  | Admin Aide III               |

**Fee: {FEE_LATE_BIRTH}, or {FEE_LATE_BIRTH_TOTAL_NOT_MARRIED} if the parents are not married. Total processing time: {TIME_LATE_BIRTH_TOTAL}.**

The charter's processing-time column gives the posting period as {TIME_LATE_BIRTH_POSTING}, while its client step says to allow 15 days before returning. Ask the office for the release date when you file.

---

## Late registration of a marriage or death

The charter lists separate services for a marriage or a death not registered within the prescribed period. Both are classified **Highly Technical**, cost {FEE_LATE_MARRIAGE_DEATH}, follow the same evaluate, pay, post and release steps, and have a total processing time of {TIME_LATE_MARRIAGE_DEATH_TOTAL}.

| Record   | What to bring                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Marriage | Four copies of the Certificate of Marriage; affidavit of the contracting parties stating their names and the date and place of marriage; affidavit of the solemnizing officer stating the exact place, date, facts and circumstances of the marriage and the reason for the delay; affidavit of two disinterested persons on the date and place of marriage, the names of the parties and the solemnizing officer; Certificate of No Record of Marriage from the PSA |
| Death    | Four copies of the Certificate of Death, duly accomplished and signed by the proper parties; PSA Negative Result; affidavit for delayed registration; authenticated copy of the certificate of burial, cremation or other means of disposal                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
