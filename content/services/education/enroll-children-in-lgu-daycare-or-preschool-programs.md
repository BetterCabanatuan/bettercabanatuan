# Enroll Children in LGU Daycare or Preschool Programs — Cabanatuan City

Barangay day care centres take children before school age, and public elementary schools take five-year-olds for kindergarten. The Citizen's Charter has no enrolment entry for parents, so this page tells you where to ask. It also gives the full charter entry for operators of child development centres, who need an ECCD Permit to Operate from the City Social Welfare and Development Office: the inspection fee is {FEE_ECCD_INSPECTION} and the total processing time is {TIME_ECCD_TOTAL}.

---

## For parents: day care

Every barangay is required by law to have a day care centre for children up to six years old ([Republic Act No. 6972](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1990/ra_6972_1990.html)). Ask your barangay hall or barangay day care centre for its enrolment schedule, age range and requirements. The charter does not publish these, and they differ by barangay.

The City Social Welfare and Development Office assesses and permits the city's child development centres (see the operator section below). If your barangay cannot tell you where the nearest centre is, call the office.

## For parents: kindergarten

Kindergarten is one year of preparatory education for children at least five years old, and it is mandatory for entrance to Grade 1. In public schools it is free ([Republic Act No. 10157, Kindergarten Education Act](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2012/ra_10157_2012.html)). Kindergarten is run by the Department of Education, not the city. Ask the nearest public elementary school for its enrolment dates and requirements, or check [deped.gov.ph](https://www.deped.gov.ph/).

## For operators: ECCD Permit to Operate

Public and private Child Development Centers (CDCs) and Learning Centers (LCs) in Cabanatuan City need a Permit to Operate from the City Social Welfare and Development Office. The charter says the prescribed period is within {TIME_ECCD_PRESCRIBED}, after the centre complies with the assessment and evaluation based on the national standard. The service is classified as **Complex**.

### Checklist of requirements

Submit one copy of each to the CSWDO Assessment Team:

| Requirement                                | Note                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Photocopy of SEC Registration              | Bring the original for presentation only                                                                                   |
| Profile of the centre                      | Location, ownership, goals and objectives, and the programme(s) to be offered                                              |
| Description and photograph of the premises | Lot size, indoor and outdoor area, number of buildings and classrooms, facilities, equipment and instructional materials   |
| Staff and enrolment list                   | Number of young children to be served, list of teachers, names of the administrator, principal or director and other staff |

The Assessment Team also asks:

1. Do the outdoor and classroom environments give access to children and adults with special needs (ramps and railings), in compliance with Batas Pambansa Blg. 344?
2. Does the centre meet current state and local building codes and safety requirements?
3. Are the windows and doors constructed to prevent injury to children?
4. Can the doors be opened inward to prevent injury to children?
5. Does the centre promote a child-friendly environment?

### The eight assessment areas

1. Health, nutrition and safety
2. Physical environment and safety
3. Interactions and relationships between staff and children, among children, and with other adults
4. Children and other adults
5. Staff qualifications, staff development and continuing education
6. Curriculum, instruction and assessment
7. Family involvement and community linkages
8. Leadership, programme management and support
