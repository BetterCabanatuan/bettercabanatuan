# Learn About Supplementary Activities for Schools in Your Area — Cabanatuan City

This guide shows where to find clubs, student organizations, competitions and enrichment programmes available to learners in Cabanatuan City.

---

## Where to look first

1. **Your school's official page or bulletin** — most clubs post tryout and enlistment dates there.
2. **DepEd Schools Division Office, Cabanatuan City** — division-wide contests, trainings and circulars. Ask your school for the division's current contact.
3. **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)** — basic literacy and Alternative Learning System (ALS) support. CSWDO Bldg., City Hall Compound. Phone: 0919 081 1345.

## Academic and interest clubs commonly found in schools

- STEM, math and science clubs: science fairs, investigatory projects, robotics
- Araling Panlipunan and social science clubs: quiz bowls, heritage activities
- MAPEH and arts: chorale, dance troupe, theatre
- TLE and ICT clubs: coding basics, multimedia, entrepreneurship
- Sports teams: tryouts announced by the school's sports coordinator

---

## Alternative pathways

- **Alternative Learning System (ALS)** — for out-of-school youth or learners who need flexible schedules. The Citizen's Charter lists ALS and basic literacy support under CSWDO; DepEd also runs ALS through its schools division.

---

## How to join

1. Scan your school's bulletin for club fair dates and adviser contacts.
2. Prepare simple requirements such as a school ID and parent consent form.
3. Attend the orientation or tryouts and note the meeting schedule.
