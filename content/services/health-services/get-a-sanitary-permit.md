# Get a Sanitary Permit — Cabanatuan City

Every food and non-food establishment doing business in Cabanatuan City, including ambulant vendors, needs a Sanitary Permit from the City Health Office's Sanitation Division. The fee is {FEE_SANITARY_PERMIT} and the permit is released in {TIME_SANITARY_PERMIT} once your requirements are complete. The permit is one of the clearances you need for a Mayor's business permit.

## Who may avail

Any person or entity engaged in any food or non-food establishment in the City of Cabanatuan, including ambulant vendors. The charter cites Presidential Decree No. 856, the Code on Sanitation of the Philippines.
