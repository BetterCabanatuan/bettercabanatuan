# Get Free Check-ups, Basic Medicines and Vaccines — Cabanatuan City

The Cabanatuan City Government offers free **check-ups, medicines, and vaccines** through the City Health Office (CHO) and the City Health Centers. The Citizen's Charter lists medical consultation, immunization, animal bite treatment, TB-DOTS, family planning and dental services as free.

---

## 1. Free Medical Check-ups

| Service                                             | Where                                      | Schedule                                                               | Requirements                                              |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| General consultation (face-to-face or telemedicine) | City Health Office and City Health Centers | Ask your health center for clinic days; charter lists 20 to 35 minutes | Individual Treatment Record (ITR) — issued on first visit |

---

## 2. Free Basic Medicines

| Service              | Where              | Schedule                          | Requirements                                |
| -------------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Free basic medicines | City Health Office | Ask the office for pharmacy hours | Doctor’s prescription from the consultation |

---

## 3. Free Vaccines & Immunization

| Beneficiary       | Vaccine/Service                                          | Where               | Schedule                                        | Requirements                                |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Children          | BCG, Pentavalent, OPV/IPV, MMR, Hepatitis B, PCV13 (EPI) | City Health Centers | Ask your health center for its immunization day | ECCD card (first-timers can get one onsite) |
| Pregnant women    | Tetanus Toxoid vaccine + supplements                     | City Health Centers | During prenatal services                        | Prenatal record                             |
| Animal bite cases | Animal bite treatment (free per charter)                 | City Health Office  | Clinic hours                                    | Valid ID                                    |

---

## How to Avail

1. **Check-ups** → Go to your City Health Center or the City Health Office; first-timers will be issued an ITR.
2. **Medicines** → Bring a doctor’s prescription/referral; submit at CHO pharmacy window.
3. **Child immunization** → Visit your City Health Center on its immunization day with the ECCD card.
4. **Pregnancy services** → Attend prenatal visits at your City Health Center for free vaccine and supplements.
5. **Animal bite** → Go to the City Health Office as soon as possible after the bite.
