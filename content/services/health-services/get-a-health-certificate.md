# Get a Health Certificate — Cabanatuan City

Anyone who works in a food or non-food establishment in Cabanatuan City, including ambulant vendors, needs a Health Certificate (health card) from the City Health Office's Sanitation Division. It costs {FEE_HEALTH_CERT} and takes {TIME_HEALTH_CERT} once you have your working permit, chest X-ray and fecalysis results. Resto-videoke bar workers need extra documents, including a Dental Certificate.

## Who may avail

Any person working in any food or non-food establishment in the City of Cabanatuan, including ambulant vendors. The charter cites Presidential Decree No. 856, the Code on Sanitation of the Philippines, which requires the certificate for employment in a business establishment.

## Dental Certificate

Office: City Health Office, Dental Division. Fee: {FEE_DENTAL_CERT}, as per City Ordinance No. 38-2010. Total time: {TIME_DENTAL_CERT}.

The charter says the Dental Certificate is required for employment in a business establishment and to determine dental age against chronological age.

**Who may avail**

1. Job applicants.
2. Children at risk or children in conflict with the law in the custody of the Cabanatuan City DSWD and the PNP.
3. Entertainment Establishment Workers (EEW).

**What to bring**

| Requirement                       | Where to get it              |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Barangay certificate of residence | Barangay hall                |
| Official receipt of payment       | Office of the City Treasurer |

**Steps**

1. Go to the triage area for a temperature check, health declaration and initial screening (5 minutes).
2. Go to the Dental Division. The dental aide receives and evaluates your requirements, takes vital signs and records them in your Patient Treatment Card (5 minutes). The dentist conducts the dental examination and prepares, signs and records the Dental Certificate (30 minutes).
3. Receive your Dental Certificate (5 minutes).

## Where to go
