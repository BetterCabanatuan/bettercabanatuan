# Go to the Local Hospital for Treatment or Confinement — Cabanatuan City

MVG Cabanatuan City General Hospital on Maharlika Highway is the city-run hospital. The Citizen's Charter lists its out-patient clinics by day, free OPD consultation, medical and medico-legal certificates, and hemodialysis. For a medical emergency anywhere in the city, call the City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office emergency line for a free ambulance response.

### OPD consultation

Who may avail: the general public, with limits during a pandemic. Fee: {FEE_OPD}. Total time: {TIME_OPD_SIMPLE} for a simple case, {TIME_OPD_COMPLEX} for a complex case.

**What to bring**

- Yourself. Personal appearance is the only listed requirement.

**Steps**

1. Go to the triage area for interview, assessment and temperature check (5 minutes).
2. Go to the Nursing Division for patient profiling and a record in your patient treatment card (10 minutes). The medical officer examines you, prescribes medicines and gives advice (5-10 minutes simple, 15-20 minutes complex).
3. Receive the available medicines and extra health instructions from the nurse on duty (5 minutes).

### Medical certificate, medico-legal certificate or medical records

For patients confined or treated at this hospital. Office: Medical Records Section. Fee: {FEE_RECORDS}. Total time: {TIME_RECORDS}.

**What to bring**

| Requirement                                      | Where to get it            |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Formal request                                   | Records Section            |
| Police or barangay request                       | Police station or barangay |
| ID                                               | Patient or representative  |
| Authorization letter, if the patient cannot come | Patient                    |

**Steps**

1. Fill up the requisition slip. Staff interview you and receive the request (10 minutes), retrieve the records and get clearance from the attending physician (10 minutes), prepare the certificate (5 minutes) and have the physician review and sign it (10 minutes).
2. Receive the certificate. One copy is kept on file (5 minutes).

### Hemodialysis

For patients with chronic kidney disease or acute kidney injury who need hemodialysis. Office: Dialysis Section. Total time including treatment: {TIME_HEMODIALYSIS}.

**Fees**

| Patient                                      | Fee                    |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| With PhilHealth                              | {FEE_HEMO_PHILHEALTH}  |
| Without PhilHealth, Cabanatuan City resident | {FEE_HEMO_RESIDENT}    |
| Without PhilHealth, non-resident             | {FEE_HEMO_NONRESIDENT} |

**What to bring**

| Requirement                                                        | Where to get it                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Endorsement letter from your present nephrologist                  | Current nephrologist                                                           |
| Medical abstract                                                   | Current nephrologist                                                           |
| Copy of your 2 latest hemodialysis records                         | Current hemodialysis clinic                                                    |
| Certification of vaccinations received                             | Current hemodialysis clinic                                                    |
| PhilHealth Dialysis Database (PDD)                                 | Current hemodialysis clinic                                                    |
| Certificate of availment of hemodialysis sessions using PhilHealth | Current hemodialysis clinic, or the hospital where you were admitted this year |
| Latest laboratory results (see below)                              | Licensed diagnostic laboratory                                                 |

Laboratory results must be recent: CBC, creatinine, potassium, calcium, sodium, phosphorus, uric acid, BUN and blood typing within 1 month; HBsAg, anti-HIV and HIV within 3 months; chest X-ray and COVID-19 rapid test (IgG and IgM) within 1 week.

**Steps**

1. Go to the OPD Dialysis Section for interview and a check of your requirements (5 minutes).
2. Go to the Admitting Section for your patient blotter (3 minutes).
3. Return to the Dialysis Section for consent, vital signs and weighing (10 minutes), then referral to the dialysis physician on duty (1 minute).
4. Physical assessment and history taking by the dialysis resident physician, who checks your laboratory results and writes the initial orders (30 minutes).
5. Preparation for dialysis in the treatment area (30 minutes), then the session itself. The charter lists the first session at 2 1/2 hours, the second at 3 1/2 hours, and later sessions at 4 hours or more.
6. Go to Billing and the Cashier to process PhilHealth documents and settle any bill (2 minutes).
7. If using PhilHealth, go to the Records Section for the PhilHealth claim form (2 minutes).
8. Return to the Dialysis Section for termination of treatment, post-dialysis care, medications and weighing (10 minutes), then discharge (3 minutes).

### Confinement

The charter does not itemise admission, ward or emergency room services at the hospital. Call the hospital at 0919 089 9880 or 044 958 9774 for admission requirements and ward availability.

## Emergency ambulance: 24/7 Emergency Response

The City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office runs a 24/7 Emergency Medical Service for vehicular and medical emergencies. The team is made up of nurses who are licensed Emergency Medical Technicians, assisted by trained first-aiders and drivers. Fee: {FEE_EMS}; you pay only your telecom provider's charge for the call. Total time: {TIME_EMS_TOTAL}, depending on location and traffic.

- Smart: 0908 881 1010
- Globe: 0917 851 1320
- Landline: 044 940 0161

**Steps**

1. Call or text the hotline. The central base answers immediately.
2. Give your name, the location, the number of patients, the vehicles involved and the patients' condition. The radio operator gathers the details and alerts the EMS team and the PNP ({TIME_EMS_CALL}).
3. Wait for the team, or call back for an update. The EMS team is dispatched to the scene ({TIME_EMS_DISPATCH}), gives first aid and transports patients to the nearest hospital if necessary.

## Other hospitals

The charter covers only the city-run hospital. Dr. Paulino J. Garcia Memorial Research and Medical Center is the Department of Health regional hospital in the city; its authorised bed capacity was raised to 1,000 by [Republic Act No. 11704](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11704_2022.html).
