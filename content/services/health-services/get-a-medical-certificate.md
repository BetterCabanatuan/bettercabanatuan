# Get a Medical Certificate — Cabanatuan City

If you are applying for a government job in Cabanatuan City, the City Health Office issues the Medical Certificate that certifies your physical fitness for employment. It costs {FEE_MEDICAL_CERT} and takes {TIME_MEDICAL_CERT} once you bring your laboratory, drug and psychological test results. Patients treated at MVG Cabanatuan City General Hospital get their medical or medico-legal certificate from the hospital's Medical Records Section at no charge.

## Who may avail

Any individual seeking employment in the government service within the jurisdiction of Cabanatuan City. The charter cites Civil Service Commission Memorandum Circular No. 34, Series of 1997, and CSC Resolution No. 97-4683.

## What to bring

| Requirement                    | Where to get it                        |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Chest X-ray                    | Diagnostic laboratory or hospital      |
| Complete blood count           | Diagnostic laboratory or hospital      |
| Urinalysis                     | Diagnostic laboratory or hospital      |
| Drug test                      | Diagnostic laboratory or hospital      |
| Psychological test             | Psychodiagnostic center of your choice |
| Duly accomplished profile slip | City Health Office                     |

Pay the fee at the Office of the City Treasurer, 1st Floor, City Hall, and bring the official receipt.

## Medical certificate from the city hospital

If you were confined or treated at MVG Cabanatuan City General Hospital, request your medical certificate, medico-legal certificate or other medical records from the hospital's Medical Records Section. Fee: {FEE_HOSPITAL_CERT}. Total time: {TIME_HOSPITAL_CERT}. Bring a formal request, a police or barangay request, your ID, and an authorization letter if the patient cannot come. Steps are on the [hospital page](/services/health-services/go-to-the-local-hospital-for-treatment-or-confinement).

## Other certificates from the City Health Office

- Health Certificate (health card) for workers in food and non-food establishments, and Dental Certificate for job applicants: see [Get a health certificate](/services/health-services/get-a-health-certificate).
