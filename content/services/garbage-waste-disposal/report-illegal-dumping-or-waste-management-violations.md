# Report Illegal Dumping or Waste Management Violations — Cabanatuan City

Dumping in public places, open burning and the other acts listed below are prohibited under Republic Act No. 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act). Report what you see to your barangay hall first, then to the Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office. The Citizen's Charter 2022 has no waste-specific complaint procedure, so the city's general complaint desk is the last step.

---

## What the law prohibits

RA 9003 Section 48 lists these acts, among others:

| Prohibited act                                                                                     | Section 48 paragraph |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Littering, throwing or dumping waste in roads, sidewalks, canals, esteros, parks or establishments | 1                    |
| Open burning of solid waste                                                                        | 3                    |
| Causing or permitting the collection of non-segregated or unsorted waste                           | 4                    |
| Open dumping or burying waste in flood-prone areas                                                 | 6                    |
| Removing recyclables set out for authorised collection, without authority                          | 7                    |
| Mixing source-separated recyclables with other waste in a collection vehicle or container          | 8                    |
| Transporting and dumping collected waste in bulk outside the facilities the law prescribes         | 13                   |

## How to report

1. **Your barangay hall.** Local governments enforce RA 9003 in their own territory (Section 10). Give the exact location, what you saw, the date and time, and any photos.
2. **Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office.** For large, repeated or unresolved cases. 2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100. Phone: 0919 081 4509.
3. **City complaint desk.** If neither office acts, use the city's general complaint channels: the Public Assistance and Complaints Desk at the City Hall lobby, text 0917 813 3247, or the Office of the City Mayor at 0919 081 3749 or cabanatuan.lgu@gmail.com.

## Penalties

RA 9003 Section 49 sets the penalties, on conviction:

- Littering or dumping in public places (paragraph 1): ₱300 to ₱1,000, or 1 to 15 days of community service, or both.
- Open burning (paragraph 3): ₱300 to ₱1,000, or 1 to 15 days of imprisonment, or both.
- Collecting unsorted waste, dumping in flood-prone areas, or unauthorised removal of recyclables (paragraphs 4, 6 and 7): ₱1,000 to ₱3,000, or 15 days to 6 months of imprisonment, or both.

Where a company commits the offence, its chief executive or officer-in-charge is liable (Section 49). The charter does not say whether Cabanatuan City has a local ordinance with its own penalties; ask the Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office.
