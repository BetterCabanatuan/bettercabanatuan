# Find Evacuation Centers and Drills — Cabanatuan City

The City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO) runs the city's evacuation planning and offers disaster-preparedness training and drills to organizations, schools and communities. The official list of evacuation centers for this site is pending confirmation from the CDRRMO, so this page gives you the office's contact and explains how to request a training or drill, which is {FEE_TRAINING} and takes {TIME_TRAINING_REQUEST} to process.

## Evacuation centers

The current list of evacuation centers is pending CDRRMO confirmation and is not published here until the office confirms it. Ask the CDRRMO which center serves your barangay, and follow the office's advisories on Facebook during a storm or earthquake.

## Request a training or drill

The CDRRMO offers these courses:

- Water Search and Rescue (WASAR)
- Collapsed Structure Search and Rescue (CSSR)
- Project Maps Orientation for Integrated Safety for Evacuation System (MOISES)
- Basic Life Support and Standard First Aid (BLS and SFA)
- Project Junior Rescuer (JR)
- Disaster Awareness and Preparedness

The charter says training may be provided through online platforms during the pandemic.

**Who may avail:** private sector, NGOs, civic volunteer organizations (CVOs), LGUs and others.
