# Call the CDRRMO Emergency Line — Cabanatuan City

If you see or are part of a road accident or a medical emergency anywhere in Cabanatuan City, call or text the City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO) emergency hotline. The line is answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The response is {FEE_RESPONSE}; you pay only your telecom provider's charge for the call. An Emergency Medical Service (EMS) team is dispatched {TIME_DISPATCH} after your call, depending on where you are and the traffic.

## Emergency hotline numbers

| Network  | Number        |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Smart    | 0908 881 1010 |
| Globe    | 0917 851 1320 |
| Landline | 044 940 0161  |

You can call or text the mobile numbers. The Central base receives the call and a Radio Operator takes your details.

## Who may call

Any individual who witnessed or is part of an accident. The charter covers vehicular and medical emergencies.

## What to say

The Radio Operator will ask for:

1. Your name.
2. The location of the accident.
3. The number of patients.
4. The type of vehicles involved.
5. The current status of the patients, as you see it.

After taking your details, the operator will tell you to wait for the EMS Team. The operator passes the information to the EMS Team for response and to the PNP for coordination and assistance.

## Who responds

The EMS team is made up of nurses who are also licensed Emergency Medical Technicians (EMTs), assisted by trained first-aiders and drivers. In 2019 the team responded to 752 distress calls and emergencies, 330 of them (43.88%) vehicular accidents.
