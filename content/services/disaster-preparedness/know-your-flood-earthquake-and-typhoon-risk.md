# Know Your Flood, Earthquake and Typhoon Risk — Cabanatuan City

Cabanatuan City sits on the upper Pampanga River and is hit by floods during typhoons and heavy monsoon rain, and the Philippine Fault's Gabaldon segment runs through Nueva Ecija near the city. This page gives the published facts about these hazards, with their sources, and points you to the official PHIVOLCS hazard map tool and the CDRRMO's own channels. It does not name specific barangays as flood-prone; the City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO) is the office to ask for that.

## Check your own location on HazardHunter PH

[HazardHunter PH](https://hazardhunter.georisk.gov.ph/) is the national hazard assessment tool run by PHIVOLCS and partner agencies. Enter your address or drop a pin to see whether your location is exposed to flooding, ground rupture, ground shaking, liquefaction and other hazards. When the CDRRMO itself asked in 2023 for a city ground-rupture and liquefaction map through the Freedom of Information portal, the request was redirected to HazardHunter ([foi.gov.ph](https://foi.gov.ph/requests/request-for-ground-rupture-active-fault-and-liquefaction-hazard-map-for-cabanatuan-city)).

## Flooding

- The city lies on the upper Pampanga River. During Tropical Storm Pepito, the CDRRMO identified 13 high-risk flood-prone barangays near the Pampanga River and Sapang Buhay Creek, and more than 700 residents were rescued ([GMA News](https://gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/regions/760879/over-700-residents-rescued-13-barangays-flooded-in-cabanatuan/story)).
- Typhoon Lando in 2015 flooded 25 barangays ([GMA News](https://gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/regions/541215/floods-hampering-rescue-ops-in-cabanatuan-city-official/story)).
- Tropical Storm Maymay in August 2026 flooded 14 barangays ([GMA Regional TV](https://www.gmanetwork.com/regionaltv/news/115085/floods-force-evacuations-as-monsoon-maymayph-batter-luzon/story/)).
- A 17-km dike to protect the city from floods was announced in 2018 ([Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/956549/17-km-dike-to-protect-cabanatuan-from-floods/)). Whether it has been completed is not confirmed by any source on this page.
- The 2015 Verisk Maplecroft Natural Hazards Risk Atlas ranked Cabanatuan 6th among 1,300 cities worldwide for natural-hazard exposure ([Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2015/03/06/1430516/headlines)).

Which barangays are flood-prone, and which are at risk in a given storm, changes with each event. Ask the CDRRMO for the current list; see the contact below.

## Earthquake

- PHIVOLCS places the Philippine Fault's Gabaldon segment in Nueva Ecija ([Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/915393/dont-just-prepare-for-big-one-phivolcs-chief-says/amp)). Gabaldon is about 40 km east of the city ([Philatlas](https://philatlas.com/luzon/r03/nueva-ecija/gabaldon.html)).
- The 1990 Luzon earthquake is one of the city's reference disasters ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabanatuan)).
- For ground rupture, ground shaking and liquefaction at your own address, use [HazardHunter PH](https://hazardhunter.georisk.gov.ph/).

## Typhoons

Typhoons Santi (2013), Lando (2015) and Karding (2022) are the city's recent reference disasters ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabanatuan)). Typhoon flooding is covered above.

## Get warnings and updates

The CDRRMO posts advisories on its Facebook page: [facebook.com/cabanatuancdrrmo](https://www.facebook.com/cabanatuancdrrmo/). The Citizen's Charter does not describe an early-warning or SMS alert service, so the Facebook page and the hotline below are the published channels.

## Training you can request

The CDRRMO offers training to the private sector, NGOs, civic volunteer organizations, LGUs and others, including Disaster Awareness and Preparedness and Project MOISES (Maps Orientation for Integrated Safety for Evacuation System). See [Find evacuation centers and drills](/services/disaster-preparedness/find-evacuation-centers-and-drills) for how to request one.
