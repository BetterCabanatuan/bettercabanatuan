# Request a Right-of-Way Certification — Cabanatuan City

Get a certification for a road right-of-way, a lot relocation or a lot survey from the Office of the City Engineer. The Citizen's Charter lists the service as free, with a total of {TIME_ROW_TOTAL}, of which one day is the field survey; a larger area takes longer. This page gives the one document you need, where to file it and each step.

---

## Who may avail

Any constituent of Cabanatuan City. The charter groups lot relocation, survey, certification for Road Right-of-Way (ROW) and other geodetic services in one entry and classifies it as **Complex** (Government to Client).

## Where to go

**Office of the City Mayor** — receives the request letter
2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 3749
Email: cabanatuan.lgu@gmail.com

**Office of the City Engineer**, Geodetic Services Division — verifies the lot and does the survey
CEO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 3694

---

## What to bring

| Requirement                                | Where to submit             |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Request letter addressed to the City Mayor | Office of the City Mayor    |
| Advance copy of the same letter            | Office of the City Engineer |

The charter asks for no other document. State in the letter which lot or road you want surveyed, relocated or certified.

| Step | What you do                                              | What the city does                                                       | Fee       | Time               | Who handles it        |

Include a contact number in your letter; the charter says the office informs you by phone call when the survey is complete.

---

## What the charter does not cover

The charter does not say what the certification is used for, which documents you receive, or whether a title or tax declaration must be shown. Ask the Office of the City Engineer at 0919 081 3694 before filing.

Reporting a damaged or blocked road instead? See [Report damaged roads, bridges, or drainage](/services/infrastructure-public-works/report-damaged-roads-bridges-or-drainage). Land titles and tax declarations are handled by other offices; see [Housing and land use](/services/housing-land-use).
