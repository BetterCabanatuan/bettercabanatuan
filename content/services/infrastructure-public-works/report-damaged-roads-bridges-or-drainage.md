# Report Damaged Roads, Bridges, or Drainage — Cabanatuan City

Ask the Office of the City Engineer to clear a road, de-clog a drainage canal, or repair a streetlight or other city-owned electrical fixture. The Citizen's Charter lists {TIME_ROADS_TOTAL} for road clearing and de-clogging and {TIME_ELECTRICAL_TOTAL} for electrical repairs, both free of charge. This page gives the one document you need, where to file it, the steps and the office to call.

---

## Who may avail

Any constituent of Cabanatuan City may request road clearing, de-clogging of drainage canals and other maintenance services. The charter classifies the request as **Complex** (Government to Client).

Repair of lighting fixtures, electrification and trouble-shooting of electrical wirings is listed for barangays and offices of the city government. If a streetlight on your road is out, ask your barangay to send the request.

## What the charter does not cover

The charter has no separate entry for bridges; damage to a bridge falls under "other maintenance services" and uses the same request letter. It lists no hotline or online form for reports, so the request letter is the only documented route. Ask the Office of the City Engineer at 0919 081 3694 before filing if you are unsure the damage is on a city road.

During a flood or typhoon, the City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office answers on Smart 0908 881 1010, Globe 0917 851 1320 and 044 940 0161.

For a certification on a road right-of-way or a lot survey, see [Request a right-of-way certification](/services/infrastructure-public-works/request-a-right-of-way-certification). Barangay officials planning a new project can [request a program of works](/services/infrastructure-public-works/request-a-program-of-works-for-your-barangay).
