# Request a Program of Works for Your Barangay — Cabanatuan City

Barangays can ask the Office of the City Engineer to prepare the plans and Program of Works (POW) for a barangay infrastructure project. The Citizen's Charter lists the service as free, with a total of {TIME_POW_TOTAL}. This page gives the one document the barangay needs, where to file it and each step.

---

## Who may avail

The charter lists the different barangays of Cabanatuan City as the clients for this service. It classifies the request as **Complex** (Government to Client). Residents who want a project in their barangay should raise it with their barangay officials, who file the request.

## What to bring

| Requirement                                | Where to submit             |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Request letter addressed to the City Mayor | Office of the City Mayor    |
| Advance copy of the same letter            | Office of the City Engineer |

The charter asks for no other document. Describe the proposed project and its location in the letter so the site inspection can proceed.

---

## What the charter does not cover

The charter covers only the plans and the POW. It does not cover funding, bidding or construction of the project, or who may sign the request letter for the barangay. Ask the Office of the City Engineer at 0919 081 3694.

A barangay that needs a road cleared, a canal de-clogged or a streetlight repaired uses a different entry; see [Report damaged roads, bridges, or drainage](/services/infrastructure-public-works/report-damaged-roads-bridges-or-drainage).
