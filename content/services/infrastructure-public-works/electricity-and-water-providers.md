# Electricity and Water Providers — Cabanatuan City

Electricity in Cabanatuan City is supplied by the Cabanatuan Electric Corporation (CELCOR), and piped water by the Cabanatuan City Water District in a joint venture with PrimeWater. Neither is run by City Hall, so new connections, bills and outages go to the provider. This page says who they are, which laws and agreements they operate under, and what the city itself handles.

---

## Electricity: Cabanatuan Electric Corporation (CELCOR)

CELCOR holds the franchise to distribute electricity in the City of Cabanatuan. Republic Act No. 9968 (2010) renewed that franchise for 25 years ([RA 9968](https://batasnatin.com/laws/ra-9968)). NEECO II, the electric cooperative, serves other parts of Nueva Ecija and not the city proper ([Inquirer, 2022](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1670621/power-outages-hit-parts-of-nueva-ecija-due-to-karding)).

This portal lists no CELCOR hotline because none was found on an official source.

## Water: Cabanatuan City Water District

The Cabanatuan City Water District is a government-owned and controlled corporation ([eFOI portal listing](https://foi.gov.ph/agencies/cabcwd)). It entered a joint venture with PrimeWater under a 25-year concession that started on 30 January 2017 ([PPP Center](https://ppp.gov.ph/?p=51258)).

This portal lists no water district hotline because none was found on an official source.

## What the city handles

The Office of the City Engineer repairs streetlights and other city-owned electrical fixtures and de-clogs drainage canals on request from barangays and residents. See [Report damaged roads, bridges, or drainage](/services/infrastructure-public-works/report-damaged-roads-bridges-or-drainage).

## What the charter does not cover

The Citizen's Charter 2022 has no entry on household electricity or water connections, billing, meter disputes or service interruptions. These are matters for the provider. If you cannot reach a provider, the Public Assistance and Complaints Desk at the City Hall lobby takes complaints by text at 0917 813 3247, and the Office of the City Mayor answers at 0919 081 3749 or cabanatuan.lgu@gmail.com.
