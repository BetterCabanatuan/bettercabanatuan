# Request Zoning, Land Use or Building Permits — Cabanatuan City

Two City Hall offices handle land use and construction permits. The City Planning and Development Office issues the zoning permit (locational clearance) first; the Office of the City Building Official then issues the building permit and, after that, the occupancy, fence, demolition, excavation and electrical permits. This page tells you which permit to start with and links the detailed pages.

---

## Step 1: zoning permit or locational clearance

**City Planning and Development Office**
2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 3219

The zoning permit is item 11 on the building permit checklist, and the Office of the City Building Official accepts an application only when the locational clearance is in it. Residential projects are Complex applications with a charter total of {TIME_LOCATIONAL_COMPLEX}. Apartments, dormitories, commercial, industrial, agro-industrial, agricultural, institutional and special projects are Highly Technical with a charter total of {TIME_LOCATIONAL_HIGHLY_TECHNICAL}. The fee is computed under City Ordinance No. 038-2010.

Full checklist and steps: [Secure a locational clearance or zoning permit](/services/business/secure-a-locational-clearance-or-zoning-permit).

If the Zoning Administrator denies your application, the charter lets you appeal to the Local Zoning Board of Adjustment and Appeals, which may also grant exceptions and variances from the Zoning Ordinance. The charter classifies the appeal as Highly Technical and says it takes more than 30 days.

### Site zoning certificate

If you only need to know how a lot is zoned, the City Planning and Development Office issues a site zoning certificate, released the same day if the property is in the city's GIS database. Bring a letter of request addressed to the City Planning and Development Coordinator, a location plan with vicinity map, a photocopy of the certified true copy of the title, the land tax declaration, the current-year land tax receipt and pictures of the site. Fee: {FEE_SITE_ZONING_CERTIFICATE}. Charter total: {TIME_SITE_ZONING_CERTIFICATE}.

## Step 2: building permit

**Office of the City Building Official**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100

The charter lists no telephone number for this office.

Required before you erect, construct, alter, repair, move, convert or demolish any building or structure. Highly Technical; charter total {TIME_BUILDING_PERMIT_TOTAL} at the office within a 15-day processing window.

Full checklist and steps: [Apply for a building permit](/services/housing-land-use/apply-for-a-building-permit).
