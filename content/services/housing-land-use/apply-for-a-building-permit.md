# Apply for a Building Permit — Cabanatuan City

Before you erect, construct, alter, repair, move, convert or demolish any building or structure in Cabanatuan City, you need a building permit from the Office of the City Building Official. This page lists the documents to bring, the steps at City Hall, the fee basis and the processing time in the Citizen's Charter.

---

## Where to apply

**Office of the City Building Official**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100

The Citizen's Charter lists no telephone number for this office. Visit in person, or ask the Public Assistance and Complaints Desk at the City Hall lobby (text 0917 813 3247).

The charter classifies this service as **Highly Technical**. Residents, businesses and government agencies may apply.

The legal basis is the National Building Code of the Philippines, [Presidential Decree No. 1096](https://www.chanrobles.com/presidentialdecrees/presidentialdecreeno1096.html).

## After the permit

Once construction is finished you need an occupancy permit before using the building. See [Apply for occupancy, fence, demolition, excavation or electrical permits](/services/housing-land-use/apply-for-occupancy-fence-demolition-excavation-or-electrical-permits).
