# Ask About Socialized Housing and Relocation — Cabanatuan City

The Cabanatuan City Citizen's Charter 2022 has no service entry for socialized housing, relocation sites or housing assistance, so this site cannot list requirements, fees or waiting times for them. This page tells you which office to ask and what national law says about relocation.

---

## Who to ask

## What national law says

The [Urban Development and Housing Act of 1992, Republic Act No. 7279](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1992/ra_7279_1992.html), is the national law on socialized housing for underprivileged and homeless citizens (Article V). Section 28 discourages eviction and demolition and allows them only in danger areas, for government infrastructure projects with funding, or under a court order. Where they do proceed, the law requires notice to the affected persons at least 30 days before the date of eviction or demolition, and adequate relocation, temporary or permanent. For court-ordered demolitions involving underprivileged and homeless citizens, relocation is undertaken by the local government unit with the National Housing Authority.
