# Apply for Occupancy, Fence, Demolition, Excavation or Electrical Permits — Cabanatuan City

The Office of the City Building Official issues five permits besides the building permit: occupancy, fence, demolition, excavation and electrical. This page lists what to bring and how long each takes, from the Citizen's Charter. All five are classified Complex, and residents, businesses and government agencies may apply.

---

## Where to apply

**Office of the City Building Official**
1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100

The Citizen's Charter lists no telephone number for this office. Visit in person, or ask the Public Assistance and Complaints Desk at the City Hall lobby (text 0917 813 3247).

Fees for all five permits are {FEE_PERMIT}. You pay at the Office of the City Treasurer (1st Floor, City Hall, 0919 089 9849 or 044 464 7696) and bring the official receipt back. The charter notes for each permit that the time may vary depending on how other offices process the application.

The legal basis for the occupancy, fence, demolition and excavation permits is the National Building Code of the Philippines, [Presidential Decree No. 1096](https://www.chanrobles.com/presidentialdecrees/presidentialdecreeno1096.html). The electrical permit follows the latest edition of the Philippine Electrical Code.

## Occupancy permit

You need this before using a finished building. The building must already have a building permit. See [Apply for a building permit](/services/housing-land-use/apply-for-a-building-permit).

### What to bring

| Requirement                                                  | Where to secure                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| One photocopy of the building permit, back to back           | The building permit issued for the structure |
| One photocopy of the Fire Certification for occupancy permit | Bureau of Fire Protection                    |
| Three sets of as-built plans                                 | You, or your Architect or Engineer           |
| One picture of the building or stall                         | You                                          |

### Steps

| Step                                                       | What happens                                                                                                 | Fee            | Time               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------- | ------------------ |
| 1. Submit the application form and requirements            | Frontline personnel review and check the set                                                                 | None           | {TIME_OCC_RECEIVE} |
| 2. Fire Safety Inspection Certificate                      | The office endorses you to the Bureau of Fire Protection; you secure the certificate there and bring it back | {FEE_BFP_FSIC} | {TIME_OCC_BFP}     |
| 3. Technical review                                        | Staff evaluate the architectural, structural, electrical, mechanical, geodetic and sanitary plans            | None           | {TIME_OCC_REVIEW}  |
| 4. Building inspection                                     | Staff check the building against National Building Code standards and electrical requirements                | None           | {TIME_OCC_INSPECT} |
| 5. Pay the permit fees at the Office of the City Treasurer | The office receives a copy of your official receipt                                                          | {FEE_PERMIT}   | {TIME_OCC_PAY}     |
| 6. Release                                                 | The Building Official verifies and approves; the permit is recorded and released                             | None           | {TIME_OCC_RELEASE} |

**Total time at the office: {TIME_OCCUPANCY_TOTAL}**, within a **{TIME_OCCUPANCY_WINDOW}** processing window.

## Demolition, excavation and electrical permits

These three permits share one checklist and the same steps. The charter describes the electrical permit as a requirement for an electric meter application: construction light, change of name and new meter connection.

### What to bring

| Requirement                                                                          | Where to secure                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Two sets of sketch plan, signed and sealed by a licensed Architect or Civil Engineer | Private Architect or Civil Engineer |
| Two copies of certified true copy of the Transfer Certificate of Title               | Register of Deeds                   |
| Two photocopies of the tax declaration                                               | Office of the City Assessor         |
| Two photocopies of real estate tax receipts for the current year                     | Office of the City Treasurer        |
| Sign board, 3 x 2 feet                                                               | You                                 |
| Barangay clearance                                                                   | Barangay                            |
| One picture of the site                                                              | You                                 |

### Steps

| Step                                                       | What happens                                                       | Fee          | Time                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------ | --------------------- |
| 1. Submit the application form and requirements            | Frontline personnel receive and check the set                      | None         | {TIME_SKETCH_RECEIVE} |
| 2. Review                                                  | Technical staff review and evaluate the requirements               | None         | {TIME_SKETCH_REVIEW}  |
| 3. Site inspection                                         | Staff inspect the site                                             | None         | {TIME_SKETCH_INSPECT} |
| 4. Pay the permit fees at the Office of the City Treasurer | The office receives a copy of your official receipt                | {FEE_PERMIT} | {TIME_SKETCH_PAY}     |
| 5. Release                                                 | Frontline personnel verify, approve, record and release the permit | None         | {TIME_SKETCH_RELEASE} |
