# Get a Senior Citizen (OSCA) ID — Cabanatuan City

Residents aged 60 and above can get an OSCA ID from the City Social Welfare and Development Office. The ID is {FEE_OSCA_ID} and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_OSCA_TOTAL}. A representative may apply for a senior citizen who is bedridden.

---

## Where to go

**City Social Welfare and Development Office**
CSWDO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 1345

Ask for the Senior Citizen Focal Person (Social Welfare Officer I).

---

## Who can apply

Senior citizens aged 60 years old and above.

---

## What to bring

- One clear photocopy of your Voter's Certification issued by COMELEC, **or** a Certificate of Residency from your barangay with dry seal and the original signature of the barangay captain.

If the senior citizen is bedridden, a representative may apply and brings:

- An authorization from the senior citizen
- The senior citizen's latest 1x1 picture and original specimen signature or thumb mark
- One clear photocopy of the representative's valid ID

---

## National context

Republic Act No. 9994 (Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010), section 6, directs the Office for Senior Citizens Affairs in every city and municipality to issue the senior citizen ID free of charge, valid anywhere in the country. Read the law at [lawphil.net](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2010/ra_9994_2010.html).
