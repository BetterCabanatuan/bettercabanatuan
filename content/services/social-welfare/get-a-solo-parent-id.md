# Get a Solo Parent ID — Cabanatuan City

Solo parents living in Cabanatuan City can get a Solo Parent ID from the City Social Welfare and Development Office. The ID is {FEE_SOLO_PARENT_ID} and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_SOLO_TOTAL}. Start with a certification from your barangay captain.

---

## Where to go

**City Social Welfare and Development Office**
CSWDO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 1345

Ask for the Solo Parent Focal Person (Social Welfare Officer).

---

## Who can apply

Solo parents.

---

## What to bring

1. Certificate from your barangay captain that you are a solo parent, have no live-in partner and receive no support from your husband, wife or partner (1 original copy)
2. Voter's ID or COMELEC certificate (1 clear photocopy)
3. Certificate or Declaration of Nullity of Marriage, if your marriage was annulled (1 clear photocopy)
4. Medical certificate for a bedridden husband or wife (1 original copy)
5. Birth certificate of each child below 18, or 18 and above but still in school and dependent on your support (1 clear photocopy)
6. Certificate of enrollment of a student under the custody of grandparents or any individual who has assumed the responsibility of parenthood (1 clear photocopy)
7. If a child has a psychosocial, mental or intellectual disability, the charter lists this as a requirement but names no document; ask the focal person what to bring
8. One 1x1 or 2x2 ID picture
9. One long white folder
10. Application form, properly filled up

Items 3 to 7 apply only to the situations they describe.

---

## National context

Republic Act No. 11861 (Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act, 2022) provides that the Solo Parent Identification Card and booklet are valid for one year. Read the law at [lawphil.net](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11861_2022.html). The charter does not state a local validity period; ask the focal person when your ID is issued.
