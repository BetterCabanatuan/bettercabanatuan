# Ask about PWD ID and medical or burial assistance — Cabanatuan City

The City Social Welfare and Development Office is the office to call about a Person with Disability (PWD) ID and about medical, burial and educational assistance. The published Citizen's Charter 2022 does not list these services, so this page gives you the office's contact details rather than requirements, fees or waiting times.

---

## Where to go

**City Social Welfare and Development Office**
CSWDO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100

- Main line: 0919 081 1345
- Educational, Medical and Burial Assistance: 0919 081 0246
- Psycho-social Intervention Unit: 0919 081 0234

---

## What is not in the charter

- **PWD ID.** The charter has no entry for PWD ID issuance and lists no Persons with Disability Affairs Office. Call the CSWDO main line to ask which office issues the ID in Cabanatuan City and what to bring.
- **Medical, burial and educational assistance (AICS).** The charter lists only the phone line above for these programmes. Call 0919 081 0246 to ask what help is available, who qualifies and what documents you need.

---

## National context

Under National Council on Disability Affairs Administrative Order No. 001, s. 2021, PWD ID cards are issued and signed by the City or Municipal Mayor and processed through the City or Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office or a Persons with Disability Affairs Office. Read the guidelines at [ncda.gov.ph](https://ncda.gov.ph/?p=11230).
