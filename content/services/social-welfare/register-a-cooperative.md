# Register a cooperative — Cabanatuan City

Groups that want to form a cooperative in Cabanatuan City can get an orientation, the Pre-Registration Seminar and Pre-Membership Education Seminar required for registration, and later a Certificate of Good Standing, from the City Livelihood and Cooperatives Development Office. All of these services are {FEE_COOP}. Each starts with a signed letter of request from your group.

---

## Where to go

**City Livelihood and Cooperatives Development Office**, Cooperatives Development Division
CLCDO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 089 9875

Hand your letter to the receiving personnel of the Administrative Division.

---

## Step 1: Orientation for would-be cooperatives

For organizations, groups and associations interested in starting a cooperative. The charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_ORIENTATION_TOTAL} to schedule and approve your orientation.

Bring:

1. Letter of request, duly signed
2. Name of the group, organization, association or cooperative, with complete address and contact number
3. List of members

---

## Step 2: Pre-Registration Seminar (PRS) and Pre-Membership Education Seminar (PMES)

The PRS and PMES are a requirement for registering a cooperative under the Philippine Cooperative Code of 2008. They are given to prospective members of existing and would-be primary cooperatives. The office prepares a letter of request for the seminar to the Cooperative Development Authority. The charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_PRS_PMES_TOTAL} from your letter to the release of your approved schedule.

Bring the same three items as for the orientation.

| Step                                                        | What the office does                                                                                                           | Time                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------- |
| 1. Submit your letter of request to the receiving personnel | Recorded and forwarded to the Cooperatives Development Division                                                                | {TIME_PRS_RECORD}   |
|                                                             | The division evaluates your documents and coordinates the seminar schedule with your group                                     | {TIME_PRS_EVALUATE} |
|                                                             | A Cooperatives Development Specialist prepares the letter of request for the PRS/PMES to the Cooperative Development Authority | {TIME_PRS_LETTER}   |
|                                                             | The head of office approves the documents                                                                                      | {TIME_PRS_APPROVE}  |
| 2. Receive your approved request and seminar schedule       | The Administrative Division releases the approved documents with the schedule attached                                         | {TIME_PRS_RELEASE}  |

The registration itself is done with the Cooperative Development Authority; the city charter does not cover the Authority's own steps or fees.

---

## After registration: Certificate of Good Standing

Cabanatuan City cooperatives that have complied with the mandatory requirements of the Philippine Cooperative Code can request a Certificate of Good Standing, signed by the City Mayor. The charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_GOOD_STANDING_TOTAL}.

Bring:

1. Letter of request, duly signed
2. Certificate of compliance
3. Financial statement

---

## After registration: capacity enhancement seminars

Cabanatuan City cooperatives can request mandatory and supplemental trainings and seminars. Bring a signed letter of request, the cooperative's name with complete address and contact number, and a list of members. The charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_CAPACITY_TOTAL} to prepare and approve a training schedule.

---

## National context

The charter cites the Philippine Cooperative Code of 2008, Republic Act No. 9520. Read the law at [lawphil.net](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2009/ra_9520_2009.html).
