# Apply for a job through PESO — Cabanatuan City

Job seekers and students can register with the Public Employment Services Office for local or overseas job matching, counselling and referrals. Registration is {FEE_PESO_JOB} and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_PESO_JOB_TOTAL}. Bring your resume, a police or NBI clearance and your employment or training certificates.

---

## Where to go

**Public Employment Services Office**
PESO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 1300
Facebook: [facebook.com/PESOCabanatuan](https://www.facebook.com/PESOCabanatuan/)

Job fairs and recruitment activities are announced on the PESO Facebook page. Check it for current dates and venues.

---

## Who can apply

Applicants for local or special (overseas) recruitment activities, and students.

---

## What to bring

For local employment:

1. Resume
2. Police clearance from the Philippine National Police station, or NBI clearance from the National Bureau of Investigation
3. Employment certificate from a previous employer, or training certificate from TESDA

For overseas employment, bring the three items above plus:

4. Passport from the Department of Foreign Affairs
5. Transcript of Records from your school
6. Picture

## For employers: holding a recruitment activity

Companies and agencies that want to recruit in Cabanatuan City apply to PESO for a Local Recruitment Activity (local jobs) or a Special Recruitment Activity (overseas jobs). Both are {FEE_PESO_RECRUIT}.

Submit these by email or in person at least one week before your target date:

1. Letter of intent
2. Company or agency profile
3. Latest job vacancies (for a Special Recruitment Activity, approved by the Philippine Overseas Employment Administration)

PESO endorses your letter to the Office of the City Mayor for approval and informs you of the date, time and venue. For a Special Recruitment Activity, PESO issues a No Objection Certificate; you then secure the Authority to Conduct a Special Recruitment Activity from the Philippine Overseas Employment Administration and submit a copy to PESO. The charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_PESO_LRA_TOTAL} for a Local Recruitment Activity and {TIME_PESO_SRA_TOTAL} for a Special Recruitment Activity, including the day of the activity itself.
