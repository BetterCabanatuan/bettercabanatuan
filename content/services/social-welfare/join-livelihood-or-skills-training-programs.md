# Join livelihood or skills training programs — Cabanatuan City

Individuals, associations, cooperatives and groups in Cabanatuan City can request livelihood skills or employable skills training from the City Livelihood and Cooperatives Development Office. Training is {FEE_TRAINING} and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_TRAINING_TOTAL} from your letter to the release of your approved schedule. Start with a signed letter of request.

---

## Where to go

**City Livelihood and Cooperatives Development Office**
CLCDO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 089 9875

Hand your letter to the receiving personnel of the Administrative Division.

---

## Who can apply

Individuals, associations, cooperatives and groups in Cabanatuan City.

---

## What to bring

1. Letter of request, duly signed
2. Name of the individual, association, cooperative or organization, with complete address and contact number
3. List of members

The charter does not list the training courses on offer. Ask the office which courses are available when you submit your request. Job seekers can also be referred to skills training or livelihood through [PESO](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-a-job-through-peso).

---

## Steps

| Step                                                        | What the office does                                                                                                               | Time                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| 1. Submit your letter of request to the receiving personnel | Recorded and forwarded to the Livelihood Management Division                                                                       | {TIME_TRAINING_RECORD}    |
|                                                             | The Livelihood Development and Management Division evaluates your documents and coordinates with the Training and Research Section | {TIME_TRAINING_EVALUATE}  |
|                                                             | The Training and Research Section prepares a training proposal                                                                     | {TIME_TRAINING_PROPOSAL}  |
|                                                             | The division recommends approval to the head of office                                                                             | {TIME_TRAINING_RECOMMEND} |
|                                                             | The head of office approves the documents                                                                                          | {TIME_TRAINING_APPROVE}   |
| 2. Receive your approved request and training schedule      | The Administrative Division releases the approved documents with the schedule attached                                             | {TIME_TRAINING_RELEASE}   |

**Fee:** {FEE_TRAINING}. **Total time:** {TIME_TRAINING_TOTAL}.

---

## For technical vocational institutes

Technical vocational institutes in Cabanatuan City that want to hold face-to-face vocational classes request a clearance from the same office. The charter lists a signed letter of request, a portfolio showing compliance with TESDA and IATF guidelines, and a Business Continuity Plan as requirements, with an inspection by the Local Inter-agency Task Force. The clearance is {FEE_TVI_CLEARANCE} and the charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_TVI_CLEARANCE_TOTAL}.
