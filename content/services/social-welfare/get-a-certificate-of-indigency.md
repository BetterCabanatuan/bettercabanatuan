# Get a Certificate of Indigency — Cabanatuan City

Students who live in Cabanatuan City can get a Certificate of Indigency from the City Social Welfare and Development Office when a school or scholarship programme asks for one. The certificate is {FEE_INDIGENCY} and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_INDIGENCY_TOTAL}. Bring a barangay indigency certificate and a photocopy of your student ID.

---

## Where to go

**City Social Welfare and Development Office**, Child and Youth Development Division
CSWDO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 1345

Ask for the Youth Focal Person.

---

## What the charter covers

The charter lists this service for three documents:

- Certificate of Indigency for scholarship assistance
- Certificate of Indigency for the Special Program for the Employment of Students (SPES)
- Social Case Study Report for scholarship assistance

Any bona fide resident of Cabanatuan City applying for educational assistance from a school or institution may apply.

The charter does not list a Certificate of Indigency for other purposes, such as hospital bills or legal aid. If you need one for a different purpose, call the office at 0919 081 1345 and ask what to bring.

---

## What to bring

- Barangay Indigency certificate from your barangay captain
- Photocopy of your student ID
