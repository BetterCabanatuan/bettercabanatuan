# Apply for a Solicitation Permit — Cabanatuan City

Organizations that want to raise funds in Cabanatuan City need a Solicitation Permit from the City Social Welfare and Development Office. The permit costs {FEE_SOLICITATION} and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_SOLICITATION_TOTAL}, including payment at the City Treasurer's Office. Bring a valid ID and a request letter.

---

## Where to go

**City Social Welfare and Development Office**
CSWDO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 1345

Submit your papers at the CSWDO lobby front desk. You pay the fee at the **Office of the City Treasurer**, 1st Floor, City Hall (0919 089 9849; 044 464 7696).

---

## Who can apply

The charter lists this permit for organizations conducting fund-raising. It is open to private individuals and groups, businesses, and government offices (G2C, G2B or G2G).

---

## What to bring

1. Valid ID
2. Request letter

---

| Step                                           | What the office does                                      | Time                        |
