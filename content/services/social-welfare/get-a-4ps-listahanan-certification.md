# Get a 4Ps Listahanan Certification — Cabanatuan City

The City Social Welfare and Development Office issues a certificate stating whether you are listed in the DSWD Listahanan II database as a Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) beneficiary or non-beneficiary. The certificate is {FEE_LISTAHANAN} and the Citizen's Charter lists a total processing time of {TIME_LISTAHANAN_TOTAL}. Get your 4Ps membership or non-membership certificate from the 4Ps Office first.

---

## Where to go

**City Social Welfare and Development Office**
CSWDO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100
Phone: 0919 081 1345

Submit your papers at the CSWDO lobby front desk.

---

## What to bring

1. Certificate of 4Ps membership or non-membership, from the 4Ps Office
2. If you are a 4Ps member: a photocopy of your 4Ps ID with three specimen signatures
3. If you are not a 4Ps member: a photocopy of your voter's ID or voter's certificate with three specimen signatures

The charter lists the 4Ps Office as the place to secure both items. The charter does not state who may avail of this service; call 0919 081 1345 if you are unsure whether it applies to you.

---
