# MVG Cabanatuan City General Hospital — Cabanatuan City

MVG Cabanatuan City General Hospital is the city-run hospital on Maharlika Highway. Its charter services are free out-patient consultation in specialty clinics on a weekly schedule, medical and medico-legal certificates and medical records, and hemodialysis for patients with chronic kidney disease or acute kidney injury.

## Location

Maharlika Highway, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 089 9880
- Landline: 044 958 9774

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Availment of Medical Services on Out-Patient Department (OPD) (pp. 271-272) — bring yourself; the only requirement is personal appearance of the patient
- Issuance of Medical/Medico Legal Certificate, Other Medical Records (pp. 272-273)
- Hemodialysis Treatment (pp. 273-276) — needs an endorsement letter and medical abstract from your nephrologist, your latest hemodialysis records and laboratory results, and PhilHealth dialysis documents

Admission, emergency care and other in-patient services are not written up in the charter. Call the hospital.
