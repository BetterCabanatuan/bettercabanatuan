# Office of the City Engineer — Cabanatuan City

The Office of the City Engineer surveys and relocates lots and certifies road right-of-way, prepares plans and programs of work for barangay infrastructure projects, clears roads and de-clogs drainage canals, and repairs public lighting and electrical fixtures. Each request starts with a letter addressed to the City Mayor.

## Location

CEO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 081 3694

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Request of Lot Relocation, Survey, Issuance of Certification for Road Right-of-Way (ROW) and Other Geodetic Services (p. 143)
- Request for Program of Works (POW) and Plans for Barangay Infrastructure Projects (pp. 143-144)
- Request for Clearing of Roads, De-Clogging of Drainage Canals and Other Maintenance Services (p. 144)
- Request for Repair of Lighting Fixture, Electrification, Trouble Shooting of Electrical Wirings and Other Electrical Services (pp. 145-146)

For every service the checklist is one item: a request letter addressed to the City Mayor, submitted to the Office of the City Mayor (pp. 143-144).
