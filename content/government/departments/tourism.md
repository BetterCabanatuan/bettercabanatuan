# City Information and Tourism Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Information and Tourism Office releases city data on request, lends a sound system, photo and video coverage and an LED wall for events, and prepares messages of the City Mayor for programmes and publications. Requests go through the City Mayor's Office for approval.

## Location

2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 089 9862

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Request for Data (p. 32) — submit a letter of intent; released after approval by the City Mayor
- Request for Sound System/Photo and Video Coverage/LED Wall (p. 32)
- Request for Message of the City Mayor (p. 33)
- Request for Sikhay (p. 34)
