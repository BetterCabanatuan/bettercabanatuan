# City Livelihood and Cooperatives Development Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Livelihood and Cooperatives Development Office gives free livelihood and employable skills training to individuals, associations and cooperatives, orients groups that want to start a cooperative, runs the Pre-Registration and Pre-Membership Education Seminars that cooperative registration needs, and issues a cooperative's Certificate of Good Standing.

## Location

CLCDO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 089 9875

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Livelihood Skills/Employable Skills Training (pp. 261-262) — submit a signed letter of request with the group's name, address, contact number and list of members
- Conduct of Orientation to Would-be Cooperatives (p. 262)
- Conduct of Pre-Registration Seminar (PRS) and Pre-Membership Education Seminar (PMES) (pp. 263-264)
- Issuance of Cooperative's Certificate of Good Standing (p. 265)
- Capacity Enhancement Seminars for Cooperatives (p. 266)
- Issuance of Certificate to Conduct TESDA Vocational Courses (pp. 267-269)
