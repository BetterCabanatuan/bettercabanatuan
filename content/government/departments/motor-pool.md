# City Motor Pool Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Motor Pool Office maintains the city's and the barangays' service vehicles, lends service vehicles and heavy equipment for city projects, re-gravels roads on request, and refills oxygen tanks for free for oxygen-dependent patients. Most services are for barangays and city offices; the oxygen refill is for residents.

## Location

City Motorpool Office Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

- Request for Pre-Repair Inspection for the Barangays with Issued Service Vehicle (p. 162)
- Request for Re-Gravelling (p. 163)
- Request for Pre-Repair Inspection for City Hall Departments with Issued Service Vehicle (pp. 164-166)
- Request for Heavy Equipment for City Projects (p. 167)
- Borrowing of Service Vehicle (p. 168)
- Request for Oxygen Tank Refill (p. 169)

## Free oxygen tank refill

The charter describes the free oxygen refill as a project of the city government "to aid its Covid19 infected citizens and oxygen-dependent patients", open to citizens of Cabanatuan (p. 169).

- Bring three copies of a request letter.
- Submit the letter to the [Community Affairs Office](/government/departments/community-affairs), which approves it and endorses it to the oxygen refilling plant.
- Fee: {FEE_OXYGEN_REFILL}. Total processing time in the charter: {TIME_OXYGEN_REFILL}, most of it the refilling itself.
