# City Social Welfare and Development Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Social Welfare and Development Office issues the OSCA ID for senior citizens and the Solo Parent ID, the Certificate of Indigency and Social Case Study Report that scholarships and SPES ask for, the 4Ps Listahanan certificate, solicitation permits for fund-raising organizations and the ECCD Permit to Operate for child development centres. It also runs basic literacy classes, ALS referrals and the Bahay Pag-Asa Residential Care Center.

## Location

CSWDO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Main office: 0919 081 1345
- Educational, medical and burial assistance: 0919 081 0246
- Psycho-social Intervention Unit: 0919 081 0234

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Certificate of DSWD Listahanan II for Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) Beneficiaries/Non-Beneficiaries (p. 283)
- Solicitation Permit (for Organizations Fund-Raising) (p. 284)
- Issuance of OSCA ID (p. 284)
- Issuance of Solo Parent ID (pp. 285-286)
- Basic Literacy for Beginners, Children at Risk Learners/Alternative Learning System (ALS) (p. 287)
- Saturation Drive/Reach Out Operation (p. 288)
- Issuance of Computer Certification (p. 289)
- Issuance of ECCD Permit to Operate (pp. 290-293)
- Issuance of Certificate of Indigency and Social Case Study Report for scholarship assistance and SPES (pp. 294-295)
- TMC Citizen's Charter (p. 296)
- Bahay Pag-Asa Residential Care Center: social case study report, admission, discharge, out on pass, progress report, referral letter, acknowledgement receipt, certificate of appearance (pp. 297-305)

The PWD ID and medical, burial and educational assistance are not written up in the charter. Call the assistance line above.
