# Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office — Cabanatuan City

The Cabanatuan City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CCENRO) issues the City Environmental Certificate that businesses need during construction and the City Environment Permit to Operate once they open, the Mayor's clearances for quarrying and tree cutting, the City Hauling Pass, and waste disposal certificates. It also receives complaints and handles violators of the city's environmental ordinances.

This is the city office. The national DENR has its own Community Environment and Natural Resources Office in Cabanatuan, which is a different agency.

## Location

2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- City Environmental Certificate (CEC) (pp. 117-120) — for business, commercial, institutional and industrial establishments at the construction stage
- City Environment Permit to Operate (CEPO) (pp. 121-126)
- Mayor's Clearance/Permit to Quarry (pp. 127-131)
- Mayor's Clearance/Certificate of No Objection/Tree Cutting (p. 132)
- City Hauling Pass (pp. 133-134)
- Liquid Waste/Residual Waste Disposal Certificate (pp. 135-136)
- Handling of Violators of Environmental City Ordinances with Penalty of Community Service (p. 137)
- Handling of Violators of Environmental City Ordinances with Penalty of Fine (pp. 138-139)
- Filing of Complaint (pp. 140-141)
