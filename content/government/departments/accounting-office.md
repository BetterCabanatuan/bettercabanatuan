# Office of the City Accountant — Cabanatuan City

The Office of the City Accountant issues BIR Forms 2306 and 2307 to the city's job order workers, consultants, suppliers and contractors, and certifies the GSIS, PhilHealth and BIR remittances and net take-home pay of city employees. Residents who are not paid by the city will rarely need it.

## Location

2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 081 3215

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Issuance of BIR Form 2306 and/or 2307 to Job Order/Contract of Service Workers and Consultants (p. 56)
- Issuance of BIR Form 2306 and/or 2307 to Suppliers/Contractors (p. 57)
- Issuance of Certification of Premium and Loan Amortization Remittances to GSIS and Taxes to BIR (p. 58)
- Issuance of Certification of Premium and Loan Amortization Remittances to GSIS (p. 59)
- Issuance of Certification of Premium Remittances to PhilHealth (p. 61)
- Request for Net Take Home Pay (p. 62)

## Feedback and complaints

Drop a feedback or complaint form in the suggestion box at the Public Assistance and Complaint Desk, City Hall lobby, or text 0917 813 3247 (charter pp. 340-341).
