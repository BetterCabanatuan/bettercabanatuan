# City Economic Enterprise and Public Utilities Management Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Economic Enterprise and Public Utilities Management Office runs the public market. Vendors get their market clearance and market certificate here, have their weighing scales calibrated, and apply for permits to hold product sampling; shoppers can report illegal vending.

## Location

CEEMPUMO Bldg., Sanciangco St., Barangay Supermarket, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 081 2785
- Email: ceepumocab@gmail.com

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Entrance for Public Market (p. 333)
- Regular Roving (p. 333)
- Issuance of Market Clearance (p. 334)
- Issuance of Market Certificate (p. 335)
- Calibration of Weighing Scales (p. 336)
- Complaint Against Illegal Vending (p. 337)
- Monitoring of Cash Ticket Collections (p. 337)
- Permits for Product Sampling (p. 338)

The charter has no service for the award or rental of a market stall. Ask the office directly.
