# City Health Centers — Cabanatuan City

The City Health Office runs six barangay-based City Health Centers and a City Epidemiology and Surveillance Unit (CESU). The Citizen's Charter's List of Offices gives their barangays and direct lines; the free consultation, immunization and other services they provide are those of the City Health Office.
