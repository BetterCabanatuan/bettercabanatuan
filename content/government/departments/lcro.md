# City Civil Registry Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Civil Registry Office registers births, marriages and deaths that happen in Cabanatuan City, issues marriage licenses and certified copies of civil registry records, and processes petitions to correct clerical errors, change a first name, and register legal instruments and court orders.

## Location

1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 081 4548
- Landline: 044 456 6920

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Issuance of Marriage License (pp. 200-201)
- Marriage Under Article 34 of the Family Code (p. 202)
- Timely Registration of Marriage (p. 202)
- Timely Registration of Death (p. 203)
- Timely Registration of Birth (p. 203)
- Delayed Registration of Birth (pp. 204-205)
- Delayed Registration of Marriage (p. 206)
- Delayed Registration of Death (p. 207)
- Issuance of Certification (Birth/Marriage/Death) (pp. 208-210)
- Petition for Correction of Clerical Error Under RA No. 9048 (p. 211)
- Petition for Change of First Name Under RA No. 9048; Correction of Birth Month and Date and Change of Sex Under RA 10172 (pp. 212-214)
- Registration of Legal Instruments: Legitimation, Acknowledgement/Admission of Paternity, RA 9255 Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father (pp. 215-217)
- Supplemental Report (p. 218)
- Registration of Court Orders/Decrees (p. 219)
- Out of Town Registration of Birth, Marriage and Death Certificate (pp. 220-222)
- Endorsement of Civil Registry Records to the Civil Registrar-General (pp. 223-224)
