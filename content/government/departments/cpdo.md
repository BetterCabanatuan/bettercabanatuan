# City Planning and Development Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Planning and Development Office issues the locational clearance or zoning permit that a business permit and a building permit both require, the site zoning certificate, and development permits for subdivisions and condominiums. It also hears appeals when a locational clearance is denied.

## Location

2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 081 3219

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Issuance of Locational Clearance/Zoning Permit (pp. 171-175)
- Issuance of Site Zoning Certificate (pp. 176-177)
- Approval of Business Permit for Locational Clearance (p. 178)
- Application for Appeal on the Denial of Locational Clearance/Zoning Permit (including Variance and Exception) (pp. 178-180)
- Issuance of Development Permit for Subdivisions and Condominiums (pp. 181-186)
