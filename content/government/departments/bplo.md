# City Business Licensing and Investment Promotion Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Business Licensing and Investment Promotion Office issues and renews the Mayor's Business Permit, issues occupational permits for workers, and records when a business retires, moves, changes its type of ownership or is transferred. Business tax is assessed and paid at the Office of the City Treasurer as part of the same application.

## Mandate

Under City Ordinance No. 075-2015 (25 August 2015), the office receives and reviews permit and licence applications, verifies the attached documents, forwards the application to the City Treasurer's representative for assessment and payment of business tax, fees and charges, prepares the business permit for the City Mayor's approval, inspects establishments on new registration and retirement, serves closure notices on unregistered and non-compliant establishments, and keeps the office's business records (charter p. 83).
