# Office of the City Assessor — Cabanatuan City

The Office of the City Assessor appraises and assesses real property for taxation, keeps the tax maps and assessment rolls, and issues certified copies of tax declarations and certifications of property holdings. If you bought, inherited or built on land in Cabanatuan City, this is where the tax declaration is updated.

## Mandate

The charter prints the office's mandate (p. 65): to execute the laws and policies on appraisal and assessment of real property for taxation; to keep a real property identification, accounting and tax mapping system; to conduct physical surveys so that all properties in the city are listed in the assessment rolls; to prepare the schedule of fair market values under the Local Government Code of 1991; to issue certified copies of assessment records on request upon payment of a service fee to the treasurer; and to attend sessions of the Local Board of Assessment Appeals.

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Issuance of Certified True Copy of Tax Declaration (p. 67)
- Issuance of Certification of Property Holdings and/or Certificate of No Property Holdings (p. 68)
- Issuance of Certification of Non-Improvement/On-going Construction of Building/Property Identification (p. 69)
- Transfer of Tax Declaration of Real Property to New Owner (Simple Transfer, Subdivision or Consolidation) (pp. 70-72)
- Appraisal and Assessment of Newly Constructed/Discovered Real Property; Cancellation of Demolished/Duplicate Real Property Units (pp. 73-75)
