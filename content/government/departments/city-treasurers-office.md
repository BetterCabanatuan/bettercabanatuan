# Office of the City Treasurer — Cabanatuan City

The Office of the City Treasurer is where you pay real property tax, transfer tax and business tax, and where you get a Community Tax Certificate (cedula) or a real property tax clearance. It also documents and brands large cattle.

## Location

1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 089 9849
- Landline: 044 464 7696

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Paying Real Property Taxes (pp. 105-106)
- Payment and Issuance of Certification of Transfer Taxes (pp. 107-108)
- Issuance of Community Tax Certificate (pp. 109-110)
- Payment and Issuance of Real Property Tax Clearance (p. 111)
- Payment of Business Tax (pp. 112-113)
- Documentation and Branding of Large Cattle (pp. 114-115)
