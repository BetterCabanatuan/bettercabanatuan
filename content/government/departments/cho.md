# City Health Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Health Office is the city's primary-level health care provider: free medical consultation in person or by telemedicine, tuberculosis and animal bite treatment, immunization, family planning and basic dental care. It also issues the health certificate and sanitary permit that workers and establishments need, medical and death certificates, and exhumation and cremation permits.

## Location

City Health Office Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100. Six barangay-based [City Health Centers](/government/departments/city-health-centers) also serve residents.
