# City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office runs the city's 24/7 emergency response line, trains barangays, schools and groups in disaster preparedness, and provides medical standby for planned events, transportation assistance, disinfection and antibody rapid testing on request.

## Location

CDRRMO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.
