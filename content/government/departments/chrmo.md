# City Human Resource Management Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Human Resource Management Office serves city government employees: service records, certificates of employment and leave credits, loan certifications, leave applications, and on-the-job training placements for students. It also collects the feedback and complaint forms dropped at the City Hall lobby.

## Location

2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 081 3135
- Email: Hrmo.lgucabcity@gmail.com

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Issuance of Service Record (p. 19)
- Issuance of Certificate of Employment (p. 21)
- Issuance of Certificate of Leave Credits (p. 23)
- Certification for Application for Loans (p. 24)
- Application for LBP Electronic Salary Loan (p. 25)
- Request for GSIS Loan Confirmation (p. 26)
- Application for Leave of Absence (p. 27)
- On-the-Job Training (OJT) (p. 29)

Job vacancies in the city government are not a charter service. Ask the office directly, or see the [Public Employment Services Office](/government/departments/peso) for job matching with private employers.

## Feedback and complaints

Drop a feedback or complaint form in the suggestion box at the Public Assistance and Complaint Desk, City Hall lobby, or text 0917 813 3247. CHRMO collects the forms daily and endorses them to the Office of the City Mayor (charter pp. 340-341).

## Source

City Government of Cabanatuan, Citizen's Charter 2022 (2nd Edition), pp. 18-30 (City Human Resource Management Office) and pp. 342-345 (List of Offices). Accessed 2026-10-11.
