# Public Employment Services Office — Cabanatuan City

The Public Employment Services Office registers job seekers and matches them with local and overseas vacancies, and approves the local and special recruitment activities that employers and agencies hold in the city.

## Location

PESO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 081 1300
- Facebook: [PESO Cabanatuan](https://www.facebook.com/PESOCabanatuan/)

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Local Recruitment Activity (p. 278) — for employers; submit a letter of intent, company profile and latest vacancies one week before the target date
- Special Recruitment Activity (p. 279)
- Application for Job Employment (pp. 280-281)
