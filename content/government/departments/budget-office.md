# City Budget Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Budget Office prepares and monitors the city budget. Its two charter services are for barangays and city offices: technical assistance to barangays on their budgets, and certification that an appropriation is available for a purchase or project.

## Location

2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 081 1400

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Technical Assistance to Barangays (pp. 77-78)
- Certification as to Availability of Appropriations (pp. 79-81)

The charter lists no service for individual residents at this office. For posted budget documents, see the [Transparency](/transparency) page.

## Feedback and complaints

Drop a feedback or complaint form in the suggestion box at the Public Assistance and Complaint Desk, City Hall lobby, or text 0917 813 3247 (charter pp. 340-341).
