# City Agriculture Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Agriculture Office certifies bona fide farmers and issues their ID cards, certifies the conversion of agricultural land, and distributes planting materials, tilapia fingerlings, soil tests, farm machinery and other inputs to farmer-beneficiaries, including rice seeds, fertilizer vouchers and financial assistance from the Department of Agriculture.

## Location

CALMO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 089 9925

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Issuance of Certification on Conversion of Agricultural to Residential/Commercial/Industrial Land (p. 188)
- Availment of Agricultural Farm Machinery & Equipment and Infrastructure Projects (pp. 189-190)
- Issuance of Certification for Bonafide-Farmers (p. 191)
- Availment of Planting Materials (Vegetable Seeds & Seedlings, Forest Trees, Fruit-Bearing Trees, Ornamental and Herbal Plants) (p. 192)
- Availment of Soil Test Analysis (pp. 193-194)
- Availment of Tilapia Fingerlings (p. 195)
- Availment of Agricultural Inputs and Other Support to Farmer-Beneficiaries (Rice Seeds, Fertilizer Vouchers and Financial Assistance from Department of Agriculture) (p. 196)
- Issuance of Identification Card for Bonafide-Farmers (pp. 197-198)
