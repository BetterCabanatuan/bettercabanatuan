# Community Affairs Office — Cabanatuan City

The Community Affairs Office is listed in the Citizen's Charter's directory but has no service section of its own. The charter names it twice: its Traffic Management Section sets the requirements for the Motorized Tricycle Operators Permit (MTOP), and it approves requests for free oxygen tank refills before the City Motor Pool Office fills them.

## Location

1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 081 3983

## Where the charter mentions this office

- Issuance of Mayor's Clearance for MTOP Application (p. 41) — the City Mayor's Office issues the clearance "in conformity with the requirements of Community Affairs-Traffic Management Section" for the MTOP
- Request for Oxygen Tank Refill (p. 169) — the Community Affairs Office receives and approves the request letter before the City Motor Pool Office refills the tank

The charter does not print the MTOP franchise steps, fees or processing time. Ask the Community Affairs Office directly.
