# City Veterinary Office — Cabanatuan City

The City Veterinary Office treats pets and livestock of Cabanatuan residents for free, gives free anti-rabies and other vaccinations, inspects meat and issues meat inspection and veterinary health certificates, investigates and responds to animal disease outbreaks, and catches and impounds stray animals.

## Location

CVO Bldg., City Hall Compound, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 089 9906

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Pet Animals Treatment (p. 307) — bring the animal and its vaccination history or previous treatment record
- Livestock Animal Treatment (p. 308)
- Availment of Free Anti Rabies Vaccination and other Vaccinations (p. 309)
- Issuance of Veterinary Health Certificate (pp. 310-312)
- Issuance of Meat Inspection Certificate (p. 313 and p. 322)
- Barangay Request for Mass Anti Rabies Vaccination, Deworming and other Veterinary Activities (pp. 314-315)
- Animal Disease Investigation and Surveillance (pp. 316-318)
- Animal Disease Outbreak Response (pp. 319-320)
- Issuance of Certification of Pathological or Post Mortem Examination for Livestock and Poultry (p. 321)
- Animal Apprehension and Impounding Activities: catching, impoundment, care of impounded animals, release, disposition, coordination with barangay officials, retrieval from barangay shelters, sharing of fines (pp. 323-330)
- Provisions of Technical Assistance (p. 331)
