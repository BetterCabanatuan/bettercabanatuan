# Office for City Legal Affairs — Cabanatuan City

The Office for City Legal Affairs gives legal assistance to residents referred by the Office of the City Mayor, receives legal complaints, helps prepare legal forms, and issues PLEB (People's Law Enforcement Board) clearances and certificates. Complaints against city personnel that go to case filing are referred here by the City Mayor.

## Location

2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 081 0213

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Legal Assistance (p. 36) — bring a referral letter from the Office of the City Mayor, your documents, and appear in person
- Issuance of PLEB Clearance and/or Certificate (p. 36)
- Legal Complaint (p. 37)
- Legal Forms Preparation and Assistance (p. 38)

## Feedback and complaints

Drop a feedback or complaint form in the suggestion box at the Public Assistance and Complaint Desk, City Hall lobby, or text 0917 813 3247. A complaint against a city employee that is not settled amicably is referred by the Office of the City Mayor to this office for case filing (charter pp. 340-341).

## Source

City Government of Cabanatuan, Citizen's Charter 2022 (2nd Edition), pp. 35-38 (Office for City Legal Affairs) and pp. 340-345 (Feedback and Complaints; List of Offices). Accessed 2026-10-11.
