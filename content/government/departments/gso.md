# City General Services Office — Cabanatuan City

The City General Services Office handles purchase requests, office supplies, logistics and manpower assistance for city offices, and issues the General Clearance. Its charter services are mostly for city departments, barangays and accredited suppliers rather than individual residents.

## Location

2nd Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

- Mobile: 0919 089 9887

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Funded Purchase Request (p. 14)
- Request for Office Supply (p. 15)
- Request for Logistics/Manpower Assistance (p. 15)
- Issuance of General Clearance (p. 16)

The charter does not list a service for residents at this office. If you need something from the City General Services Office, call the number above or ask at the Public Assistance and Complaint Desk in the City Hall lobby.

## Feedback and complaints

Drop a feedback or complaint form in the suggestion box at the Public Assistance and Complaint Desk, City Hall lobby, or text 0917 813 3247 (charter pp. 340-341).

## Source

City Government of Cabanatuan, Citizen's Charter 2022 (2nd Edition), pp. 13-17 (City General Services Office) and pp. 342-345 (List of Offices). Accessed 2026-10-11.
