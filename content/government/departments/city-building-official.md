# Office of the City Building Official — Cabanatuan City

The Office of the City Building Official issues the permits you need before you build, occupy, fence, demolish, excavate or wire a structure in Cabanatuan City: the building permit, occupancy permit, fence permit, demolition permit, excavation permit and electrical permit.

## Location

1st Floor, City Hall, Kapitan Pepe Subd. Phase II, Cabanatuan City 3100.

## Contact

The Citizen's Charter's List of Offices prints no phone number for this office. Visit the 1st Floor of City Hall, or ask at the Public Assistance and Complaint Desk in the lobby.

## Services in the Citizen's Charter

- Issuance of Building Permit (pp. 148-150)
- Issuance of Occupancy Permit (pp. 151-152)
- Issuance of Fence Permit (pp. 153-154)
- Issuance of Demolition Permit (pp. 155-156)
- Issuance of Excavation Permit (p. 157)
- Issuance of Electrical Permit (pp. 158-160)
