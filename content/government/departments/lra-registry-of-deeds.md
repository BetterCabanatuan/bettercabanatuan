# Land Registration Authority — Registry of Deeds — Cabanatuan City

The Registry of Deeds is an office of the Land Registration Authority, a national agency, not a department of the City Government of Cabanatuan. It keeps land titles and registers deeds, mortgages and other instruments affecting land. It does not appear in the city's Citizen's Charter, and City Hall does not handle title transactions.

## Contact

The Citizen's Charter prints no address or number for this office. Find the Cabanatuan City Registry of Deeds through the Land Registration Authority's website at [lra.gov.ph](https://lra.gov.ph/).

## What the city does handle

Title transfer at the Registry of Deeds is separate from the city steps that come before or after it:

- [Transfer a tax declaration to a new owner](/services/taxes-and-certificates/transfer-a-tax-declaration) at the Office of the City Assessor, which requires the transfer tax paid at the Office of the City Treasurer
- [Get a real property tax clearance](/services/taxes-and-certificates/get-a-real-property-tax-clearance)
- [Request a certified true copy of a tax declaration](/services/taxes-and-certificates/request-a-certified-true-copy-of-tax-declaration)
