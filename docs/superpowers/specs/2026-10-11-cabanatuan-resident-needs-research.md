# Cabanatuan Resident Needs Research

**Date:** 2026-10-11

**Status:** Research findings

**Scope:** What Cabanatuan City residents most commonly need from the city
government, and which portal features and content that implies, grounded in
primary sources (the city's own Citizen's Charter and website, PSA, DILG, ARTA,
DICT, statutes) and in what peer LGU portals ship.

All URLs below were accessed on 2026-10-11. Anything not traceable to a primary
source is marked **unverified**. No official's name, phone number or address is
recorded unless it is quoted from an official document, with the source given.

## Summary

1. The single most useful primary source is the city's own **Citizen's Charter,
   2022 (2nd Edition)**, a 345-page text-layer PDF on cabanatuancity.gov.ph. It
   names all 25 departments, their City Hall addresses and phone numbers, and
   gives requirements, fees and processing time for ~230 services. Most of the
   portal's empty categories can be filled from it today.
2. The official site is largely a shell: every item under "Services" and
   "Contact Us" resolves to `maintenance.php`; hotline and office-contact pages
   are images with no text. A text-first, searchable restatement of the charter
   is the portal's clearest value.
3. Highest-volume resident transactions (by charter coverage and national
   standards): business permit new/renewal (2 days / 1 day), real property tax
   (quarterly, 10-20% discounts), cedula, civil registry certifications
   (P50/doc), OSCA ID (free), solo-parent ID, health certificate and sanitary
   permit, locational clearance, building/occupancy permits, and the
   Cabanatuan-specific **MTOP (tricycle) mayor's clearance**.
4. Emergency: the charter publishes CDRRMO hotlines (Smart 0908 881 1010,
   Globe 0917 851 1320, landline 044 940 0161) and a 24/7 free EMS. The repo's
   `hotlines.ts` has a different Smart number (0918-…) and unverified PNP/BFP
   numbers; reconcile before shipping.
5. Hazard profile is severe: Pampanga River flooding (13-25 barangays per
   event), typhoons, and the Philippine Fault's Gabaldon segment ~15 km away. A
   2015 global atlas ranked the city 6th most hazard-exposed city worldwide.
6. Compliance baseline for an LGU site: Citizen's Charter online (RA 11032 s.6),
   Full Disclosure Policy documents (DILG MC 2024-021, 14 core documents),
   Transparency Seal items (GAA FY2026 s.112), a Data Privacy notice (RA 10173
   s.11/16), WCAG 2.0 (DICT MC 2017), and Filipino-language availability.
7. Static content fits almost everything above. Online payment, appointments,
   queueing and document tracking need a backend; the realistic path is linking
   to DICT's eLGU/eGovPH or the city's own system once confirmed.
8. Utilities correction: Cabanatuan's electricity distributor is **CELCOR**
   (RA 9968 franchise), not NEECO II. Water is Cabanatuan City Water District in
   a 25-year JV with PrimeWater since 2017.
9. bettergovph has no LGU template repo; this project's closest siblings are the
   `naga` AIP dashboard and the `hotlines` PWA (451 entries, zero for Nueva
   Ecija), both contribution targets.

### Hospitals

The charter lists the city-run **MVG Cabanatuan City General Hospital**,
Maharlika Highway, 0919 089 9880 / 044 958 9774, with OPD and free
hemodialysis services (Citizen's Charter pp.270-276, 345). The DOH regional
hospital **Dr. Paulino J. Garcia Memorial Research and Medical Center** had its
authorised capacity raised from 400 to 1,000 beds by
[RA 11704 (2022)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11704_2022.html).
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabanatuan) also lists Eduardo L.
Joson Memorial Hospital (provincial), Immaculate Conception Medical Center,
Nueva Ecija Doctors' Hospital, GoodSam Medical Center, Premiere Medical Center,
Wesleyan University General Hospital and Cardiovascular Center, and Cabanatuan
Doctors Medical Center (under construction); treat these as secondary. The
repo's `src/data/hospitals.yaml` has six entries including a "Medical City
Clinic" that no source here confirms.

### Transport

Cabanatuan Central Transport Terminal is the main bus terminus; Maharlika
Highway (AH26) and the Nueva Ecija-Aurora Road to Baler pass through; a
temporary CLLEX Cabanatuan exit opened March 2026 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabanatuan),
**unverified**). The city site's project gallery lists flyovers, bypass roads
(Tramo-Pedro Manubay, SM City-Emilio Vergara Highway), dredging (Sumacab Este,
M.S. Garcia) and Sapang Buhay Creek works
([index.php](https://www.cabanatuancity.gov.ph/index.php)).

### Utilities

- Electricity: **Cabanatuan Electric Corporation (CELCOR)**, franchise renewed
  for 25 years by [RA 9968 (2010)](https://batasnatin.com/laws/ra-9968) covering
  the City of Cabanatuan. NEECO II serves other parts of Nueva Ecija, not the
  city proper ([Inquirer 2022](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1670621/power-outages-hit-parts-of-nueva-ecija-due-to-karding)).
  No CELCOR hotline found from an official source.
- Water: **Cabanatuan City Water District**, GOCC on the
  [eFOI portal](https://foi.gov.ph/agencies/cabcwd); JV with PrimeWater,
  25-year concession from 30 Jan 2017 ([PPP Center](https://ppp.gov.ph/?p=51258)).
  No hotline found from an official source.

### Recurring hazards

- **Flooding.** The city sits on the upper Pampanga River; CDRRMO identified 13
  "high-risk flood-prone" barangays near the Pampanga River and Sapang Buhay
  Creek during TS Pepito ([GMA](https://gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/regions/760879/over-700-residents-rescued-13-barangays-flooded-in-cabanatuan/story)).
  Typhoon Lando (2015) flooded 25 barangays ([GMA](https://gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/regions/541215/floods-hampering-rescue-ops-in-cabanatuan-city-official/story));
  TS Maymay (Aug 2026) flooded 14 including San Juan Accfa
  ([GMA Regional](https://www.gmanetwork.com/regionaltv/news/115085/floods-force-evacuations-as-monsoon-maymayph-batter-luzon/story/)).
  A 17-km dike was announced in 2018 ([Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/956549/17-km-dike-to-protect-cabanatuan-from-floods/));
  completion **unverified**. The 2015 Verisk Maplecroft Natural Hazards Risk
  Atlas ranked Cabanatuan 6th among 1,300 cities for natural-hazard exposure
  ([Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2015/03/06/1430516/headlines)).
- **Earthquake.** PHIVOLCS leadership places the Philippine Fault's Gabaldon
  segment in Nueva Ecija ([Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/915393/dont-just-prepare-for-big-one-phivolcs-chief-says/amp));
  Gabaldon is about 40 km east of the city ([Philatlas](https://philatlas.com/luzon/r03/nueva-ecija/gabaldon.html)). CDRRMO's 2023 FOI request for a
  city ground-rupture/liquefaction map was redirected to PHIVOLCS HazardHunter
  ([foi.gov.ph](https://foi.gov.ph/requests/request-for-ground-rupture-active-fault-and-liquefaction-hazard-map-for-cabanatuan-city)).
  The 1990 Luzon earthquake and Typhoons Santi (2013), Lando (2015) and Karding
  (2022) are the recent reference disasters ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabanatuan)).
- **Evacuation centres.** A NEUST-reposted list names Lazaro Francisco
  Integrated School, Nueva Ecija High School, Araullo University, St. Nicholas
  de Tolentine compound, NEUST Gen. Tinio campus, Midway Colleges, San Jose NHS,
  and CIC Sumacab Este ([NEUST](https://cpadm.neust.edu.ph/tag/evacuation-sites));
  undated, **unverified**.

## 3. Most-demanded resident transactions

### National standards that set expectations

- [RA 11032](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2018/ra_11032_2018.html)
  s.3 covers "all government offices and agencies including local government
  units"; s.6 requires a Citizen's Charter on billboards, websites and printed
  material "in English, Filipino, or in the local dialect", listing the
  checklist of requirements, procedure, responsible person, maximum processing
  time, fees and complaint procedure; s.9 caps processing at 3 / 7 / 20 working
  days (simple / complex / highly technical); s.11 requires LGUs to automate
  BPLS or set up an electronic Business One-Stop Shop; s.7 sets a zero-contact
  policy.
- DILG-DTI-DICT JMC 01 s.2016 (Revised BPLS Standards): 3 steps, 1-2 days for
  new permits, 1 day for renewals, unified form
  ([DILG CALABARZON](https://news.calabarzon.dilg.gov.ph/?p=1674);
  [DTI](https://www.dti.gov.ph/archives/regional-archives/region-2-news-archives/bpls-coaches-meet-for-the-implementation-of-the-revised-bpls-standards/)).
- DILG MC 2019-177: barangay clearance for business permits is applied for and
  issued at the city BOSS, per RA 11032 s.11(f)
  ([batasnatin](https://batasnatin.com/laws/mc-2019-177)).
- Senior citizen ID: OSCA "in all cities and municipalities" issues the ID and
  purchase booklet free ([RA 9994 s.6 and IRR](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2010/ra_9994_2010.html)).
- PWD ID: issued and signed by the city/municipal mayor, processed through the
  C/MSWDO or PDAO (barangay captains are not issuing officials); NCDA AO 001
  s.2021 ([NCDA](https://ncda.gov.ph/?p=11230)).
- Solo parent ID: RA 11861 (2022), issued through the C/MSWDO, valid one year
  ([batasnatin explainer](https://batasnatin.com/laws/solo-parents-welfare-act-ra-11861);
  statute text not fetched).
- Community Tax Certificate: Local Government Code RA 7160 ss.156-164
  ([lawphil](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html);
  statute, not fetched here).

  copy.

  once Cabanatuan's onboarding is confirmed (not found in any source here).
